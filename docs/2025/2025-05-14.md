---

After our rest in the crypt, we call out to Olanthius, but there's no response. We think it probably won't help to stay around.

Our recent fight means have acquired a number of war machines.

On the map of the flying fortress, what seems to be the command deck, at the top of the structure, has a circle of writing on it. Lunghiss uses Comprehend Languages to read the writing. However, Lunghiss cannot translate it, as they are runes. But *(Arcana check)* he recognises them as divination runes. To the right, is a small room with a summoning circle.

On the Third Deck are more runes, which serve a similar purpose of divination.

We think the best strategy is to use the Arches of Ulloch to teleport directly to Zariel's command deck.

---

We ask Lulu for her advice. Lulu says "The sooner we can get the sword back to Zariel, the better."

"Have you been in Zariel's fortress?"

"No, I haven't."

We plan to drive our vehicle to the Arches, disable the vehicle, then hopefully transport ourselves directly to Zariel's command deck, giving us the element of surprise.

As we get into our vehicle and head to the Arches, we see in front of us on a peak to the west of the crypt someone impaled. We approach to see the form of Olanthius: he looks now completely dead, as opposed to undead. We remove him from the tree. As we lift him off the tree, we see his heavy armour has been punctured by the tree's spikes. The remains inside the armour look mummified and blackened.

Norgreath uses Speak with Dead and asks Olanthius what has happened.

"Can you tell us what happened to you since we last met?"

"I was judged."

"Is there any way we can save you now?"

"I have been damned for many years."

"We go to meet Zariel: do you have any advice for us?"

"Run."

"We have the sword: is there any advice for us now?"

"Strike quickly."

We have one question remaining.

"If we are set on redeeming Zariel, how should we do this?"

"Appeal to her previous nature."

Olanthius dies again.

Both Galen and Barrisso bless him. As they do so, we hear a familiar yet terrifying sound: the sound of Haruman, Lord Knight of the Far Hills, lieutenant of Zariel, who [we met before](../2024/2024-09-11.md). He appears riding his nightmare steed.

Karthis casts Haste on Gralok.

Gralok uses Form of the Beast, and attacks with his claws, doing 21HP of slashing damage: with Infectious Fury, he does 39HP of damage.

Lunghiss casts Shadow Blade, then does a sneak attack with Savage Attacker, and does 37HP of damage on Haruman, plus 12HP of thunder if he then moves.

Haruman tries to frighten anyone in range *(60 feet)* but fails. He then attacks Lunghiss three times and downs him.

Gralok uses a reaction but misses.

Norgreath attacks with Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast) three times, the first with Genie's Wrath: only the first attack hits, doing 12HP of damage.

The nightmare tries to hit Gralok, but misses.

Barrisso attacks Haruman with the sword of Zariel, doing 19HP: he adds Divine Smite, making it 43HP of damage.

Galen casts Heal on Lunghiss, reviving him and giving him 70HP of health.

Karthis casts Shatter at Haruman, using Power Surge: Haruman takes 24HP of damage, the horse takes 37HP of damage.

---

Gralok attacks with his bite, doing 30HP of damage.

Lunghiss attacks Haruman, doing a sneak attack with Savage Attacker with his Shadow Blade, doing 40HP of damage (plus 10HP of thunder damage if he moves).

In his turn, Haruman seems to heal himself, knitting his armour back together.

Norgreath attacks with Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast) three times, the first with Genie's Wrath, doing 26HP of damage.

The nightmare attacks Barrisso, doing 16HP of damage.

Gralok gets a reaction attack, doing 4HP of damage.

Barrisso attacks with the sword of Zariel, doing 25HP of damage with his first attack. Using Divine Smite, he adds 32HP of radiant damage. His second attack, with Luck, does 16Hp. Using Divine Smite, he adds 25HP of radiant damage. Haruman screams and is enveloped in the radiant energy.

He calls out to his mistress "M'lady, your blade! Your blade" and dies.

---

Galen casts Sacred Flame at the nightmare but it saves.

Karthis casts Shatter, doing 29HP of damage.

Gralok attacks with his claws, killing the beast.

---

We search Haruman's body. He wears plate armour of Infernal iron, and carries a lance and great sword. The nightmare wears Infernal tack. Strangely, Barrisso notices the lance and tack are magical, but nothing else is. We take the magical items.
