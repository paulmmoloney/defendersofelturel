# 4 Dec 2025

**[Previously](2025-11-26.md):** The party has traced the first element of the Tyrant of War’s key to a manor on island of Cardaeram. In a running battle, the party engaged three Champions as arrows flew and early spells failed under Tycho’s anti-magic field. A towering blue warrior and his elite allies joined the fight, striking brutally and poisoning several of us while Barrisso was cut down. We counterattacked with steel and limited magic, but many spells dissipated as Gralok and Norgreath pushed forward. Barrisso was healed but repeatedly struck down as Tycho directed concentrated fire, and Gralok endured heavy wounds. Our casters tried operating outside the anti-magic zone with mixed success while Isolde, Torvan, and Evano overwhelmed Norgreath and Barrisso. Gralok eventually fell to combined ranged attacks, and the enemy tightened control of the battlefield as we struggled to hold our ground.

---

Barrisso fails his death saving throw (his first fail).
Jackson is bloodied (below half HP).
The thief/rogue is bloodied (below half HP).
The assassin is untouched.
We managed to land some arrows on the other three, but none are bloodied.

Lunghiss opens his Eversmoking Bottle, it obscures the corridor in all directions, 60’ radius.
He then moves down the corridor and around the corner with a cunning action.

Tycho has been enveloped in smoke, but knows it came from the corridor in front of him.
He calls out, with an instruction to his squad “Sight gone, defensive pattern delta.”
We hear five echoing voices, “Sight also gone.”
We don’t hear any movement: we think they stayed close to their positions.

Norgreath casts Shadow of Moil to become obscured. As a bonus action, he flies out the window, heading for the ground floor window where they came in.
Karthis casts an alchemist fire to the corner of the door, and runs down the corridor as far as he can.
We hear a female voice, and a vial smashing against metal.

The barbarian rolls 19 on a death save.
He considers re-rolling to try and get a 20, but decides against it.

The cleric walks down the corridor away from the melee as far as he can.
He feels Karthis in the corridor, and moves past him.
He makes it around the corner.

We hear voices, “Trying to approach”, "I'm on fire”, “Drop and roll”.
And we hear some muffled rolling as they try to put out the alchemist’s fire.

Taq moves down the corridor around the corner out of the smoke area.

We hear “Ivano moving by Turvan, next to Tycho now.”
We hear “Jackson in position, still no vision.”

The paladin fails a second death save.
He spends an inspiration to roll again, and succeeds.

Lunghiss has an open Eversmoking Bottle, so he closes the bottle with his action.
He moves ahead down the corridor out of the zone.

Tycho calls out “Can you come now?”
“Yeah, I’m getting up.”
“Move back in the room.”

Norgreath flies over the roof, and sees on the outside some archers assembling.

Karthis says “Stay off the floor, traps are set.”
And then moves to catch up, outside the smoke.

Gralok does a death save, and fails.
With inspiration he rolls 19, succeeding.
That’s two successes.

Galen moves to join the others.

We are not sure what the enemies do on their turn.

Taq stays with the party.

Barrisso rolls with a luck point.
His death save and succeeds – two successes, one failure.

Lunghiss looks for direction.
Karthis suggests going out a window.
He goes to the balcony where we came in.

Norgreath flies down to where he can see Lunghiss, but is around 50’ away.

Karthis gets to the window.

Gralok rolls a success (with inspiration) on a death save.

Galen moves to the balcony.

We hear some noises down the corridor.
A shout “I have vision, move towards the north west”
“I have vision too”, “I have vision”.

Taq joins them on the balcony.

Barrisso fails a death save, but gains an inspiration, third success.

Lunghiss goes to the edge of the balcony, fixing a rope, dropping it down into the courtyard.

The enemies are communicating, but not shouting.

Norgreath flies down to the bottom of the rope.

Karthis climbs down the rope.

Gralok saves, so is stable on 0 HP.

Galen comes down the rope.

Taq flies down and scouts towards the exit in the west, through a closed gate.
There’s an exit over the wall.
Or we could enter the building through the door.

Norgreath casts Invisibility (Limited Wish) on four characters.
We use a rope to traverse the wall.
We make our way back to our hideout, staying invisible.
We walk slowly to reduce the chance of Galen making a noise.

As we walk around we see the 5 archers in the square, communicating with folks in the building we’ve left.
We skirt around the outer walls to avoid crossing this square.

In the end though it took us so long to navigate the crowded streets that we were running out of invisibility.
We use some subterfuge to blend in (Hat of Disguise for Lunghiss, minor illusion of a blue mask for Norgreath) and we make it back to our safe house.

Back in the fort, the paladin revives. He’s bound, gagged and shackled, and within the zone of antimagic. They seem to be in a dungeon area behind a barred door with a bolt. Tycho is getting his wounds ministered to, and has a seat and a desk, and is given some food.

He asks if anyone else has been killed. They report no one else has been hurt, but that no one has managed to locate the other enemies as yet.

Barrisso revives and feigns unconsciousness. Gralok is more vocal and asks for cheese. Barrisso and Gralok both realise their possessions are gone. They see their equipment is in the room, stacked out of reach, but within the antimagic zone.

Tycho sits and stares at his prisoners. One of his crew stands behind his shoulder and she whispers. She is the assassin.

They ungag Barrisso.

He asks: “What did you hope to achieve?”
Barrisso: “We wished to kill you. We wish to restore magic to this world.”
Tycho: “That’s gone well.”
Barrisso: “That’s the first skirmish in a bigger war.”
Tycho: “What world are you from?”
Barrisso: “Toril”.
Tycho: “What interest has Toril in our world?”.
Barrisso: “None, but I do. I need to remove the item you hold as we need it to remove an evil much more important than anything here.”.
Tycho: “You and he will die, as you have attempted a coup in this world, and there’s a penalty for that.”.

They re-gag Barrisso, and throw hoods over their heads.
It feels like they stay in the antimagic field.

Back in the safe house, the remaining party take a long rest.
They plan to use Taq to scout again.
Karthis and Taq take the watch.

Barrisso and Gralok are brought into the open air.
They are executed at dawn, their heads are chopped off.
At no time were they outside the anti-magic zone.

There are two spells we could use to revive our fallen comrades:

- Raise Dead: Need all body parts (500 GP diamond)
- Resurrection: Closes all mortal wounds and restores missing body parts (1k GP diamond)

Galen will prepare the latter [during the long rest.](2025-12-10.md)
