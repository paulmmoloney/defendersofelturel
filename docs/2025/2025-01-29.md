After the long rest, Norgreath re-summons his familiar, Taq. Taq recalls that he was last killed at the Modron ship by a large insectoid, that came from its depths and crunched him.

We can return to Fort Knucklebone either by a bridge over the Styx, or by teleportation via the Arches of Ulloch. We choose the Arches. On the way, we take damage from environmental effects. We arrive at the Arches, thinking about Fort Knucklebone. The Arches hum, and teleport us to just outside Fort Knucklebone. The kenkus are probably still back at the Modron shop, so we decide to check out the market. There's an apothecary, a gourmand, a soul broker, and scribes with scrolls, maps, and spells. Barrisso the noble paladin glares at the soul broker. 

Barrisso asks a redcap where Mad Maggie is. It clomps over to us and soon a verbal fight breaks out. Eventually he says: look, she's in her usual accommodation, and scuttles off. We head over to Maggie's abode, and knock on the door for her attention. The door seems to be made of wood, but the sound is much more metallic. Lunghiss *(Nature check)* can't determine why the sound does not match the appearance. We knock again, and this time a redcap opens the door. It stomps out, slams the door behind it, looks up at us and snarls "WHAT???". We ask to see Mad Maggie, to tell her more stories about our adventures on Avernus. "Ohhh!" he says, returning inside. A while later, the redcap reappears. 

"What are you doing here?" 

"We're waiting for Maggie, to tell her more stories of the realms." 

"I told her." 

"Did she say anything?" 

"No. No. No no no."

We ask him to tell Mad Maggie that we have tales we can pass on to her. He agrees, but tells us not to hang around. We leave.

---

We go to a tavern in the market. They ask for gold, which is curious (rather than soul coins). Or silver, or platinum, or electrum. Barrisso ask the proprietor how much for a drink that the redcaps enjoy, as he wants to make up for the fight earlier. The tavern owner is not interested in mere currency: he wants a picture made from coins. 

Barrisso offers some information instead; he tells him the story of how we freed the unicorn from the demon zapper. But the proprietor does not seem impressed by this. Barrisso tells the redcap it's hard to get him a drink. After some discussion, the redcap gets his beer, and Barrisso asks that he hopes they are now friendly again. And wonders if he can intercede with Maggie on his behalf. The redcap asks for more beers. Barrisso puts out 11 silver pieces, and arranges them into the shape of one of the Vanthampur family. The proprietor sweeps up the coins, and offers the redcap four beers, who quaffs them with abandon. 

Soon, Mad Maggie comes to the market area. "Ah, you've come", she says to us. "Let's find out what you found out."

We tell her we have all the components of the dream machine. "Some of my boys are out gathering things, but will be back soon."

As usual, she gathers us in a circle, puts out some skulls, and lights some candle. Barrisso *(Arcana check)* thinks she is performing a ritual which he cannot discern. He think the skulls record information, or that there is a 'zone of truth' involved.

We tell her of our recent tales, such as restoring the titan's true form, freeing the dao, and going to the Modron ship for a Nirvanan cogbox. 

"Oh, you met my boys there! Were they nice to you?" We say we expected them back here.

Mad Maggie wraps up the ceremony, gathers up the skulls, then says "Ah, there we are" as the war machines arrive back toFort Knucklebone, including the kenkus Chukka and Clonk. Lunghiss asks them about the demon machine, and how long it will take to repair. "Tomorrow?" We bid them farewell.

---

We decide to check out the scrolls and potions stalls in the market. The spell scrolls stall shows us some spell scrolls: Fault Line, Avronin’s Astral Assembly, and Icy Manipulation. We buy all three, for a soul coin each.

The second stall is for potions and once-off scrolls. They show Barrisso a scroll of summoning for a domovoi: a fey creature that enjoys violence and bone-breaking. Useful as enforcers, they resemble large stony goblins with mossy beards. The proprietors lean over to Lunghiss and offer him a flask of oil: Locksmith's Oil. It can be used to give a +D4 to picking locks. The third item they offer an Oil of Sharpening: it can be applied to a piercing/slashing weapons to make critical hits easier. We wonder how much the items cost. We offer Red Ruth's skull necklace, and they accept it for all three items.

Karthis needs a shard of ice for one of his spells. The stallholder offers him one if we can barter. We have a steel bottle which contains a Breath of Elemental Air in return. We decide to take a long rest until the next day. 

---

During the long rest, Norgreath meditates, and feels a presence. He recounts the stories of our exploits to his patron. He tells the patron about the concept of soul coins here, and wonders can he transfer one to him. He hears an answer in his mind: "Yes. The means to do so is already in your possession." Norgreath recalls a mysterious coin that [Lunghiss once pickpocketed from Red Ruth](../2024/2024-10-30.md) and is [not of this universe](../2024/2024-11-06.md), and wonders if this can be used to transport items to his patron. 

Norgreath places a soul coin on the mysterious coin, then returns to his trance state and regains communication with his patron. The message that comes into his brain is "You must follow the path." He touches the mysterious coin. He feels the coin pulling on him, and also send out tendrils to all his companions. He tries to stop concentrating, but fails. A moment later he finds himself on a path of luminous silver, with cool air all around. The ribbon supporting him alternates between reflective and transparent. An undertow drags him along the surface. He follows the path forward. He sees many confusing fleeting images of things beneath him. He keeps walking, feeling the path is taking him to his patron. He finds a path winding down to what seems like solid ground, or at least, a beach. He hears the sound of high waves crashing in the bay. He realises all traces of the tendril path have disappeared.
