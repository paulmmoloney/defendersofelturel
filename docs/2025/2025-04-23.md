We decided to return to Olanthius, to see if he can be of any help with us redeeming Zariel. 

Bazelsteen's gaze follows us as we head off towards the Crypt of the Hellriders. The party take some psychic damage from the environment. We are then pelted by a rain of stones and heat, but escape unscathed. We reach the Crypt. We see a trail of war machines heading in our direction from the direction of the dock. 

Galen disables our war machine so, hopefully, it can't be stolen. Norgreath leaves Taq outside to keep watch. We want to create barricades to block the enemies' way, but there's nothing we can use that isn't sacrilegious. 

Lunghiss casts Snare, to block the front door, while Barrisso casts Magic Circle. 

Taq reports: "Three war machines have landed, with twelve devils emerging. Four of them are weird humanoid-devil-scorpions, on top of which are crystalline-looking creatures. There are also spined devil, but they look... furry?"


Lunghiss holds his action: to melee attack any creature which gets caught in his Snare, or manages to pass it.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast, and Genie's Wrath on the first bolt)) at the rider of the first mount, doing 35HP damage.

Gralok rages, then fires his (unmagical) bow at the same creature: one shot hits.

Barrisso attacks the rider with the sword of Zariel but misses.

One of the scorpion mounts crosses Lunghiss's Snare. It is hoisted magically into the air, and restrained until the spell ends in 1 minutes. The creature thrown off the mount lands on its feet.

Lunghiss attacks, doing 35HP of damage (and 10 more if the creature moves).

Another scorpion creature moves up to cut down their ally. But since the restraint is magical, that does not work. Instead it backs off.

Another scorpion creature moves up. This one makes an Arcana check on the Snare, but fails, then moves out of the way. 

The last scorpion mount moves up and also makes an Arcana check, and also fails.

Karthis casts Shatter against the enemy group, doing 29HP of thunder damage against two riders, 14HP against another two. The four mounts all take 14HP of damage.

A furry spined devil flies over and attacks Barrisso, doing 4HP of piercing damage, plus 5HP of poison damage.

A second furry spined devil attacks Gralok, but misses.

A third and fourth furry spined devil move up.

Galen casts Bless on the party.

A devil casts a Darkness spell that covers the whole area.

Lunghiss attacks, doing 35HP of damage (and 9 more if the creature moves).

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast, and Genie's Wrath on the first bolt), injuring the hanging creature.

Gralok's attack finishes it off.

Barrisso attacks with his sword of Zariel, doing 32HP of damage with his first attack. His second attack finishes the creature off. Barrisso retreats back.

We hear a guttural voice in Infernal, crying out "It _is_ here! They _do_ have it! We must destroy them."

A scorpion mount charges towards us, doing 2 stinger attacks on Barrisso, doing 19 damage.

Karthis casts Haste on Barrisso.
