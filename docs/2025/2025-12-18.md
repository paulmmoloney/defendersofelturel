# 18 Dec 2025

**[Previously](2025-12-10.md):** After a disastrous attempt to attack Tycho in his fort, the party had to retreat and lost Barrisso and Gralok, who were both executed. Norgreath and Karthis managed to retrieve their heads in order to perform a resurrection. Galen then casts Resurrection on Barrisso. The next morning, a blue-skinned man appears at our hideout: his name is Crom Picket. He is one of the magic users being hunted: he mentions his daughter is at the valley where they have been making a last stand. To aid us, he gives us six vials each of serpent venom and wyvern poison. 


---

Galen needs a long rest to recover the resurrection spell. After a long rest, we revive Gralok.

Lunghiss, wearing the Hat of Disguise, wanders the city listening for news.
He discovers folks are a bit more relaxed, without a recurrence of violence.
Some of the shops still seem closed and shuttered.
We don’t seem to be in direct danger of discovery in our hideout.

We evaluate our equipment – Karthis transcribes some spells.

The Crom helper returns and gives us some equipment, and food and drink for a few days, including a nice selection of cheese and wine.

We spend a few days recovering the levels of exhaustion of our two fallen comrades, Barrisso and Gralok.

We plan how we should make our attack to win the artifact.
We think Galen should cast Temple and Word of Recall so we can return to there within 24h.
Then we can use Norgreath’s ring to get to the battle, and use Galen’s Word of Recall to retreat when needed.
We think we shouldn’t do this until the night before Gralok would be fully recovered.

In the mean time we send Taq to explore the building where we were.
He sees circa 100 militia in a cordon around the building.
Inside, the rooms where we had a fight he found nothing – there are some militia, but no leader and no companions.
He listens at the mess hall and discovers the leader is absent, maybe outside the city.

Similarly, he re-scouts the other fortress, no sign of our main foes.
There are some locked chests that may contain our lost equipment – we’re keen to recover magic items and weapons that Gralok and Barrisso were carrying if we can locate them.
We can try to use the Locate Object spell when Galen is in range.

Here Taq listens into another local conversation.
We discover the leaders have most likely gone to the valley!
They seem to have mustered some troops and gone on a military expedition.

This changes our plans!
We need to come to defend our friends from renewed attack [as soon as we can.](../2026/2026-01-07.md)
