# Epilogue: One Year Later

**[Previously](2025-05-22.md):** The party infiltrated Zariel's flying fortress and successfully persuaded her to change her mind about the choice she made to Asmodeus's argument to many years ago. Her angelic form is restored in a explosion of light and we escape the doomed fortress on Lulu's back (now full size) and in Norgreath's ring. Zariel flies to Elturel, where the planetar we met before emerges from the Companion. She severs the chains which hold the floating city, while the planetar raises it up, taking it and us back home from Avernus to Faerûn. Zariel gives us a single golden feather, in case we ever need aid if Asmodeus takes retribution. The last we see of Zariel and Lulu is their forms flying into the sunset.

## Galen

Galen would go back to Elturel an help the people of the city get their lives back together, healing and restoring those with the greatest need. All the while fighting the cultists that hindered our investigation into what happened to Elturel (before we found Zariel's contract). After a year, it is obvious that city will never be restored to it's former glory and a cleric on his own can't really do much combating those who would sow chaos. Galen how has has to choose, stay in Elturel helping the wretched population or move, maybe to Baldur's Gate, where he can join a Temple of Tir and fight with others against the forces of chaos.

## Norgreath
With my character
Norgreath knows he will gain access to powerful Warlock spells at advanced levels, particularly Limited Wish at 14th Level and Wish at 17th Level. There are many other powerful spells available (almost one new Mystic Arcanum spell per new level) such as Force Cage or Plane Shift at 13th Level, Dominate Monster at 15th Level.

Therefore, he does not wish to retire, but to continue active engagement to gain experience and level to achieve these powers that have motivated him since he first took the Warlock’s path. 

Exposure to the evil in Avernus has solidified his good alignment. He is happy to help Barrisso and future good-focused tasks. He’ll become more religious, and pray to the god of Barrisso (not sure who this is) and/or Galen, giving thanks for their divine rescue. 


Norgreath will use his status as a saviour of Elturel, and as a noble, to find a position of influence in Elturel itself and in Baldur’s Gate – he’ll establish a teleportation circle in each location and will seek to learn the symbols needed to establish some other circles e.g. Waterdeep. He’ll have a base (doesn’t need to be fancy – maybe rooms permanently leased in a friendly tavern) in both Elturel and Baldur’s Gate – he’ll think it’s premature to have a more major base like a castle or a manor house, until he reaches 17th Level.

He will work to rebuild a strategic alliance between Elturgard and Baldur’s Gate cemented by a common allegiance to the Lord’s Alliance. He will tell a narrative of the recent tensions as coming from the poison that was introduced in Elturel by the devils’ pact, that has now been broken, with the city returned to the prime material plane, and Zariel restored to her angelic form.  He’ll actively engage with the Lord’s Alliance and try to recruit other members of the party, especially Barrisso (due to his role) and Karthis (due to his family connection) in joint campaigns supporting this agenda.

Norgreath will continue to build the relationship with his genie patron, visiting him and giving him as many of the “spare” treasures the party no longer needs, such as the various components of the hell machines, and the soul coins (a Nirvanan cogbox, a heartstone from a hag, astral pistons and Phlegethosian sand). He will explain that he had no opportunity to secure the sword, without fighting Zariel in her angelic form, which he could not hope to do.  

He’d be happy to do some solo adventures, or small adventures, with his party in search of some exotic items for his patron, but he will not let this undermine his basic good alignment.

Norgreath will also seek to rebuild a link to his own people, the elves of Karthis’ background, and ideally establish a teleportation circle there as well. He will try and establish a clear line of communication, via his noble status, with the elves and half elves there.

## Gralok

Gralok has left the Hellriders having become disillusioned with its hierarchical structure (mainly due to holier than thou white eyed flying as......).

He left Baldur's Gate and Elturel to start a farm (a cheese making dairy farm) inland from the sword coast.

Not being a clever man and only having a basic understanding of the nature of hell, he smuggled some abyssal chicken eggs from avernus in order to have 'some tasty eggs' to go with his breakfast cheese.

This has had a number of ramifications for him:

- He is covered in a mass of scars and has lost an eye (abyssal chickens being much more difficult  to farm than actual chickens).
- It turns out that there is a market for abyssal chicken meat and that they are difficult to keep (see previous point).
- As a result, he has had much success as the largest exporter of (abyssal) poultry on the Sword Coast.
- His customers are generally a bit problematic, individuals with a taste for abyssal meat tend to have 'colourful' pasts and a pressing need for secrecy.

In the short term, he is concentrating on his farm and ensuring that his competitors do not get a foothold in  the abyssal market. This is less of an issue than he thinks, they rarely find much of the "poultry rustlers" who attempt to steal from his farm (occasionally they find some inedible scraps of left in the poultry shacks).

In the long term, he constantly talks about returning to the 'cold north' to regain his primal nature. This talk is mainly pub talk and thus he is concentrating on drinking himself into nightly oblivion and (unsuccessfully) working on his 'anger management issues'.

Plus given that he is a chicken magnate, he has grown a van dyke beard, wears a white suit all the time and has taken to calling himself the colonel as that was his rank in the Hellriders (that's what he remembers anyway)!

## Lunghiss

In his downtime, Lunghiss is taking time out to go full time into the art forgery business, but misses the life of adventure. He helps some of the orphaned kenkus of Baldur’s Gate and other towns by teaching them the tricks of his trade. Sometimes he reminisces fondly to them about his adventure in Avernus and how they saved Elturel; some of his audience, inspired by his tales, decide they should become adventurers too. For those who are discreet, he teaches them the secret of his other trade, recommending that a good blade and a few spells on the side are a good combination to have in any fight.

## Karthis

Once the party returned home, Karthis announced that he was dedicating the next year to eliminating the curse which are elemental resistances, and in particular, fire resistances. He bought a stone tower with a large basement which was perfect for his experiments.

The key breakthrough was reducing flasks of oil to a tar-like consistency and combining with an Elemental Bane spell. Having produced a large quantity of the test liquid, he locked himself in the tower and began testing by coating wooden models with the oil and testing the damage from a fireball spell.

People in the nearby village reported that an explosion shook the ground and the fire in the ruins of the tower burned for days.
