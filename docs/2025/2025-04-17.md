# 17 Apr 2025 

**[Previously](2025-04-09.md):** We are attempting to enter Zariel's flying fortress, in order to confront Zariel herself. Barrisso disguised himself as a bone devil, and parlayed with a horned devil named Bazelsteen. The devil agreed to allow us inside, as long as we test-drove his experimental Stygian bathysphere. Unfortunately during the test, the Stygian dock was attacked by a band of vrocks. Bazelsteen left the bathysphere controls to fight them, and the bathysphere's cable was snipped by a glabrezu demon. Fortunately, those inside used a combination of Norgreath's ring (Genie's Vessel) and Dimension Door to escape back to the dock, and there kill the glabrezu demon. The party is now in battle with the vrocks, and it's Taq's turn.

Taq hides.

Galen casts Sunbeam at a vrock, doing 27HP of radiant damage, and blinding him.

Norgreath, flying over the river, casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast, and Genie's Wrath on the first bolt) at the vrock engaged with Gralok. 2 bolts hit, doing 24HP of damage.

Karthis moves towards the dock, and while maintaining Haste, casts Ice Storm at three of the cold-resistant vrocks, doing 18HP of damage to two of them, 8HP to one.

Bazelsteen and his devil comrades now attack the vrocks. The vrocks retaliate: one hits Gralok, doing 7HP damage.

---

Lunghiss attacks a vrock with his magicked shortbow, doing 25HP of damage including sneak attack damage.

Barrisso attacks the vrock next to Gralok with his halberd, but misses.

Gralok attacks a vrock with his claws (Form of the Beast); his second attack does 5HP of damage. That vrock (Infectious Fury) in turn lashes out at another vrock. His other attacks mean a total of 14HP of damage.

Galen moves, then attacks 4 vrocks in a row with his Sunbeam. Two take 27HP of damage (and are blinded), two take 13HP of damage. The two who take full damage die. 

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast, and Genie's Wrath on the first bolt); attacks kill a vrock. One remains.

Karthis casts Magic Missile, doing 15HP of force damage to the vrock. 

Bazelsteen attacks the vrock with his fork, and finishes him off, tossing the body into the Styx.  

---

We hail Bazelsteen: "Well, that was interesting!". Bazelsteen is angry about the demons.

"What happened to the bathysphere?"

"A glabrezu cut the chain, but fortunately we escaped. Can you retrieve it?"

"Not immediately, no." 

Norgreath casts Summon Aberration to conjure up a slaad (a large frog-like bipedal from Limbo) which he hopes to send down to the bathysphere where he can attach a chain or rope. Barrisso makes a deal with Bazelsteen that we will retrieve his bathysphere in return for the floor plans of the flying fortress. 

Norgreath commands the slaad to bring a rope down to the bathysphere (Galen first casts Bless on it) and tie the rope to the severed chain on top of the bathysphere. Once it does that, it should swim back up.

The slaad dives into the river, finds the bathysphere, and successfully attaches the rope. At the other end of the rope, the party feel a tug, signifying it has been attached. The party pull on the rope to bring the chain to the surface. After a few moments, the slaad resurfaces too. 

Bazelsteen sidles over, sweeps his wings back, and uses his fork to lift the chain. He brings it to the chain hanging from the lift mechanism, and shouts to his barbed devils. They scurry away, and return with a box. He takes a bar from the box and hurls flames at it until it glow, then lifts it with his bare hands and bends it in a link that joins both chains together. They use the lift mechanism to raise the bathysphere from the river bed. 

Bazelsteen hands us a series of deck plans of the flying fortress: twelve decks in total. There are several entrances:

- A rooftop hatch on the flight deck
- The sole intake pipe at the level of the Styx
- One-foot signal vents: access pipes for imp couriers
- The lower perches where devils launch from the the fortress, on the fourth and eleventh deck

We ask him about the winner of the contest: "a deal is a deal", Bazelsteen says, giving a soul coin to Norgreath.
