# 10 Dec 2025

**[Previously](2025-12-04.md):** We performed chaotic retreat as several of us fell into death saves until Lunghiss filled the corridor with smoke from the Eversmoking Bottle, letting us slip away under cover. With Tycho’s squad blinded and holding position, we scattered: Norgreath cloaked himself in Shadow of Moil and took to the air, Karthis hurled alchemist’s fire to slow pursuit, and the rest of us withdrew down the corridors, stabilizing our fallen where we could. Hearing the enemy struggling with fire and trying to regain vision, we regrouped on the balcony, fixed a rope, and climbed down into the courtyard while Norgreath scouted above and later cast his Limited Wish invisibility on us. We avoided archers assembling outside, skirted the square, and blended into the city once our invisibility waned, finally slipping back to our safe house. Meanwhile, our captured comrades Barrisso and Gralok were interrogated by Tycho, held in an antimagic field with their gear out of reach, and eventually executed at dawn despite their attempts at deception. As the rest of us rested and planned our next move, Galen prepared Resurrection, knowing we would need it to bring them back.

---

Lunghiss tries to identify the poisons that were used to attack us, but fails miserably.

To locate the bodies of Barrisso and Gralok, we sent Taq in to investigate. He finds the heads of our slain comrades on spikes on a bridge. Taq also notices there are a series of armed patrols sweeping through the east side of the city. They are checking houses for something. If someone doesn't answer the door, they break it down. The patrols take up to ten minutes to thoroughly search each house. Taq assumed that they will reach our base eventually.

Lunghiss locks the door. We all board Norgreath's ring, which Taq will carry. Sometime later, a patrol comes by. They try the locked door, and shout inside. They look inside the building, then bash their way into the building from the rear. They eventually leave: Lunghiss comes out of the ring and checks there are no patrols in the vicinity. He communicates this back to the party in the ring: since he doesn't speak, he mimics the words of a famous bard. The rest of the party then leave the ring. We wait until darkness.

---

The plan is to retrieve the heads of Barrisso and Gralok: that's the minimum we need to resurrect them. Both Norgreath and Karthis can fly. Lunghiss casts Invisibility on both of them. Karthis picks up a coin: it disappears. So anything they pick up - including the heads - will also become invisible.

Norgreath and Karthis fly to the bridge, to see the gory sight of his friends' heads on spikes. Many people mill around on the bridge, including militia. Norgreath asks Taq to distract them with a thrown pebble. They grab the heads and return as quickly as possible to their base.

Galen casts Resurrection, first on Barrisso. He is initially weakened, with four levels of exhaustion. We rest and take watch, which passes uneventfully.

Taq has been scouting the bridge area: some chaos has erupted there, and more defences have been erected. The heads been stolen has rattled the militia.

---

The next morning, there is a knock on the door. Taq sees a blue-skinned man in a long cloak wearing a backpack and holding a staff. Karthis casts Alter Self on himself, to look like a blue-skinned human. While the rest of the party hide, he opens the door.

The man is around 50, with straggly hair and beard. Karthis notices that the tips of his left hand's fingers are stained.

"Yes?"

The man is startled. "Can I come in?"

"Yes."

He scuttles inside.

"Are you the only person here?"

"What is it you want?"

"I want to help."

"What do you think you're helping."

"We haven't had people here in this place for two and a half years. Are you _that_ sort of person?"

"That sort?"

"You know....."

"Speak plainly."

"Can.. can you summon spells?"

"I can."

"Did you cause that kerfuffle on the island?"

"I did."

"Are you OK?"

"Fine, thanks."

"Not all of you are OK."

"True. Why do you want to help us?"

"Unity is not the right way."

Karthis summons the rest of the party.

"Argh! The angel has pointy ears. And that's a bird person!"

"Have you got any spellcasting ability?"

"My daughter had. I hope she is still in the valley."

Barrisso relates how the party attacked Tycho, and some were killed.

Barrisso uses Insight, and think this man is honest.

He tells us how him and his allies have resisted the rise of Unity. He thinks he has 50 allies that he can trust - and more that he cannot.

We tell him we need to defeat Tycho and recover an artifact. One problem is we need to gather some equipment first. Barrisso needs a suit of scalemail, scimitar, and longbow. Gralok needs scalemail and perhaps a greataxe. We mention we may need some poisons.

At this point, he takes his backpack off and places on the ground. It clinks. He takes out two different sorts of vials. One type look like wide-necked potion bottles. Six bottles have a symbol of a two-legged dragon. The other six have an image of a snake. They are serpent venom and wyvern poison.

We thank the man for his help. He tells us his name: [Crom Picket.](2025-12-18.md)
