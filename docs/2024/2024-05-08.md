# 8 May 2024

**[Previously](2024-05-01.md):** We've been transported from Candlekeep to the city of Elturel, currently split into two and anchored above the fiery plains of Avernus, where we rescued a woman, Harkina, and her two children from a band of devils. We made our way east towards the High Hall of Elturel, where crossing one of the only two bridges to link the city together, we dealt with a band of devils guarding it, then rescued three shield dwarves from a collapsing building. They have joined our motley band, and we continue east across the bridge.

- [ ] Investigate Thavius Kreeg, High Overseer of Elturel
- [ ] Find the other half of the Infernal contract between Kreeg and Zariel
- [ ] Free Elturel from Avernus

---

Karthis *(Religion check)* thinks that runes on the bridge can be activated, presumably as a defensive mechanism. The runes remind him of the god Torm.

Barrisso approaches the bridge to look at the runes. They are carved into the bridge. Barrisso walks to the middle of the bridge: it seems safe. He checks a rune with his finger: he feels something, but something that is missing. Barrisso (although he's not his god) prays to Torm: the runes activate and glow. He thinks that any fiend or devil that comes through the runes will suffer radiant damage.

The party crosses the bridge and gets its bearings. We need to go a short distance west, then south, to get to the High Hall.

---

Along the way, we get a sweet chemical smell, perhaps of perfume, from a building. The building's windows and doors are all heavily locked and barred. Norgreath sends his familiar Taq in spider form in to check the building out.

Inside the building is a shop with a floor of cracked glass. The broken glass leads us to think it was an apothecary. The shop contains an intact cask behind a scorched wooden counter. Taq changes to an imp and flies around. A back room looks like it was barricaded which was then torn down. The front door is still locked.

Taq goes upstairs. Three rooms, two bedrooms and a study, all ransacked, but no one or no bodies here.

Suddenly, a human girl wearing dark robes covered in silver sigils approaches our group outside the building. Karthis asks her "Do you need any help?"

"My father has been captured by bearded devils in the south of the city!"

The party decides to follow her back to the bridge. As we walk south, a large ape-like fiend comes into view. Before we can react, the child throws up her hands, murmurs something arcane, and between us and the ape is a wall joining the two buildings either side of us, protecting us from the ape. Karthis and Lunghiss recognise the spell as Silent Image.

She brings us to a house. "They're being held in here. Can you help me?"

Norgreath asks her what is her most useful talent. "I don't know. I might be able to freeze one in place. Or make you all look like devils?"

She casts it on the whole party, including the dwarves, but excluding Lulu, the hollyphant.

Norgreath sends Taq in to scout. The house seems quite upmarket, similar to Vanthampur Manor. Drawing rooms, dining room and kitchen, with a large staircase leading up. The manor is quite damaged inside. The entrance hall has ornaments and statues that have been ransacked.

In the back sitting room are a gathering of thirteen humans, a half-elf and a dwarf, shoved behind a turned-over table, acting like a pen. In front of the table are three Bearded Devils.

As we wait outside, a group of Spined Devils plus a Barbed Devil come around the corner. We decide to enter the house to avoid them.

The devils inside hear us coming in. One of the Bearded Devils sticks his head out from the back sitting room into the hall, and asks in Infernal "Who are you guys?"

"We were sent up to round up the humans."

"They're our prisoners!"

"Where's that written down?"

Barrisso steps forward and says "Zariel sent us. Are you questioning her authority?"

The devil twigs that we are actually in magical disguise. He calls out "THERE ARE FALSE DEVILS IN THE HALL" and attacks us, but misses.

The second devil attacks Barrisso with beard and glaive - the glaive hits, for 4HP of damage.

The third devil stays in the back room.

Our young magical companion casts a spell, but the devil saves.

Gralok attacks a devil with his mace and shield, hitting for 10HP of bludgeoning damage. His second attack does 6HP.

Lunghiss does a Booming Blade sneak attack with Savage Attacker on the same devil, doing 28HP of damage, plus 4HP of thunder damage if they move.

Norgreath attacks the same devil with Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast) and Genie's Wrath, doing a possible 11HP of damage, felling the devil.

Norgreath attacks another devil, but misses.

Barrisso attacks the second devil with his rapier twice, doing 9HP of piercing damage.

Karthis casts Toll the Dead at the second devil, but nothing happens *(they save)*.

Galen casts Toll the Dead: it takes effect, and does 8HP of damage.

The second devil attacks Barrisso with beard and glaive: both miss.

The third devil emerges from the back room. Behind him we hear the captured humans shouting. "HEY, GET US OUT OF HERE!" The third devil attacks Gralok and misses.

Our young companion is figuring out something in the background, but not attacking the devils.

---

Gralok attacks with his mace twice, doing 16HP of bludgeoning damage.

Lunghiss does a Booming Blade sneak attack with Savage Attacker on the same devil, doing 33HP of damage, plus 8HP of thunder damage if they move. He's toast.

Norgreath attacks the third devil with Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast) and Genie's Wrath, doing 12HP of damage.

Barrisso attacks the third devil with his rapier twice, doing 22HP of damage. He uses Divine Smite, doing an additional 12HP of damage. Finally, his shortsword does 6HP of damage. The third devil dies.

---

We stash the three corpses, as there are devils still outside. We tell the humans via the girl that we are actually non-devils in disguise.

Suddenly there's a banging on the door. Barrisso opens the door. "What are you doing?"

A barbed devil at the door asks "Are you bringing out the prisoners, or what?"

"We'll bring them out soon."

"Give us a break."

One of the other devils says "What did you do with my mates?"

"We sent them back to the top of the hill."

The devil goes "Oh... OK."

The barbed devil turns to the bearded devil and says "I thought you said they were ready to go?"

"I want them. NOW."

We know there is a hierarchy, and a barbed devil is higher than a bearded devil.

Barrisso asks the barbed devil that he has something to tell him in private.

"We were sent here to keep the humans alive and bring them in gently."

"Who told you?"

"I don't know his name. He was wearing captain's stripes."

The barbed devil appears to believe Barrisso.

"We'll get the guys out, don't worry.

"Do you need to take them all?"

"That's what I was told to do."

"Surely we can bring some of them."

The barbed devil eventually agrees with us to keep all the humans.

Barrisso says he will commend him to his superiors.

---

We hope we can distract the devils, so that we can bring Lulu out from the house, perhaps flying out via the upper stories.

We bring the humanoids out from the house and march away with them, hoping that Taq and Lulu will follow us in the air.

**Lysandra**(the young companion) asks us where the party can go. We say we only know the safe place might be the High Hall.

"What about the vampire?"

"What vampire?"

"He's back, the old High Rider."

This must be High Rider Klav Ikaia, [as in the tale recounted to us by Reya Mantlemorn.](2024-04-17.md#mantlemorn)

"Where is he?"

"He has been hiding in a temple. There are devils and demons and vampires in this city. However, the head vampire is trying to keep alive as many humans as he can. The Hell Riders who died here have risen as Hell Knights. They are scouring the city for other Hell Riders that they can slay and turn into followers of Zariel. There is a remaining force of people from the Prime Material plane, such as the Duke of Baldur's Gate. He may be in the High Hall. But walking through the streets with so many people is dangerous. We need to get them off the street."

We decide to look for a defensible building where we can leave the innocents we have gathered. Lunghiss picks the lock of a large building, big enough for the three dwarves and other humanoids. We give them some of our rations, telling them we will investigate the High Hall and return to them later. Norgreath casts Create Food and Water, enough to sustain them with food for a day and water for longer. [We continue on our way.](2024-05-22.md)
