Of the four devils, three have been turned by Galen. Only the chain devil is lucid, and the party focus their attacks on him for now. 

Lunghiss attacks the chain devil (using inspiration for advantage) with his short bow, doing less than the 27 damage he rolls.

Gralok runs to the chain, and climbs up to reach the chain devil. He then throws a javelin, but misses.

The chain devil attacks Gralok, doing 19HP of damage to Gralok. 

Barrisso fires two arrows at the chain devil, but both miss.

The three charmed devils don't do anything.

Karthis moves, then casts Shatter so only the chain devil is impacted: he does 8HP of damage.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast), with Genie's Wrath: the first kills the chain devil. The second devil takes 14HP of damage.

Galen casts Toll the Dead at the injured devil, doing 19HP of necrotic damage: the devil dies. Two bearded devils remain.

---

Lunghiss moves next to a devil, but holds his action (so that he can sneak attack).

Gralok moves next to the devil: Lunghiss' attack misses.

Gralok attacks using Form of the Beast with his claws, doing 10HP of damage.

Barrisso jumps down from the chain to land beside the devils. He attacks with Radiant Blade and short sword, doing 9HP of piercing damage.

Karthis casts Toll the Dead on the same injured devil, but does not damage.

The devils now retaliate.

The first devil does a beard attack but misses, then picks up its discarded glaive.

The second devil does a beard attack but also misses, then picks up its discarded glaive.

Norgreath moves, then casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast), with Genie's Wrath: both miss.

Galen moves, then attacks with his +1 Marble Mace, using its pool of energy to do 5HP of extra radiant damage. He does 11HP of damage, killing the devil. One remains.

---

Lunghiss attacks the devil, but misses, then retreats.

Gralok attacks using his claws, doing a total of 19HP of damage.

Barrisso attacks with his Radiant Blade twice, then his short sword: he does 16HP of damage.

Karthis casts Toll the Dead: he takes 6HP of necrotic damage.

The last devil attacks Barrisso with beard and glaive, doing 8HP of damage. 

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast), with Genie's Wrath, doing 23HP of damage. It dies.

---

On the bodies, we find a gem of unknown value. We head back towards Fort Knucklebone, following the bank of the river Styx. A hail of meteors strike the vehicle: he take refuge underneath. The rain of stones intensifies, with blazing orbs plummeting from the sky. The party take a max of 24 damage.

Galen casts Prayer of Healing, giving everyone in the party 18HP of healing back. Gralok gets extra healing as his health is so low.

---

As we drive along the Styx, we find a pile of bones, seemingly deliberately gathered from a battlefield. 

We pass a broken-down vehicle which we saw before, and from which we took salvage. 

The party is affected by the extreme heat of Avernus, but take no damage.

We pass another war machine impaled on a jagged spike of stone. Strange green demon ichor dribbles from it, but not enough to collect. There seems to be nothing of value, so we continue on.

The party again is affected by the extreme heat of Avernus, but take no damage.

We near to Bel's Forge, avoiding the crucified figures along the river there, and continue on to arrive at Fort Knucklebone. 

---

We meet our old friend, Mad Maggie. "It's good to see you! I've heard you've been up to LOTS of things." 

She sits us down, as is her custom, placing her collection of skulls around us. 

We tell her of our recent adventures: Uldrak the Tinker, the crypt of the Hellriders, releasing the spirits, Arkhan the Cruel, Red Ruth, and so on. When we finish, we feel a strange weight lifting off us, now that we have recounted our story. Mad Maggie says: "You didn't bring Ruth's skull, by any chance? No? That's a pity. She could have joined my collection. Are you going to give the heartstone to Chukka and Clonk? You should."

We give the heartstone to the pair to the kenku pair: Galen feels that the Dream Machine is now mostly repaired.

The party take a long rest.
