# 8 Nov 2023

[**Previously**](2023-11-01.md): So, your caravan of refugees have made it to the Basilisk Gate of Baldur's Gate where the gateway to the city has been gated by a certain Zodge of the Flaming Fist.

You signed a deal with him and have become deputised to deal the Dead Three Cultists in the city and in return your caravan is allowed past the gate and into the city beyond.

---

We shepherd our refugees through the Basilisk Gate. We're not sure if the Elfsong Tavern has rooms. Only Barrisso has been in Baldur's Gate before, and only briefly, where he had stayed in the Helm & Cloak.

Once through the gate, Zodge calls us aside. "That's my side of the deal done. Here's the facts. Half a dozen bodies have been dumped in Insight Park. That's in the Brampton area near Cliffgate. We know it's Dead Three related as ritual symbols were carved into the bodies. Or, it could be someone putting us on a false trail.

You are authorised to kill those responsible on sight."

Norgreath asks if we have access to the upper city. Zodge wonders why we need to go there. Norgreath says the perpetrators might be there. Zodge accedes. He tells us to meet his informant, Terina. We go to the Elfsong Tavern, to find lodgings and to seek Terina.

*[Everyone goes up to Level 2]*

----

In the Elfsong Tavern, we notice that the patrons are all armed and armoured. There are two bouncers: a female half-ogre, and an animated suit of armour.

Gralok asks the ogre: who is the suit? "**Clank**" is the answer.

Next to the couch is a selection of games: card decks, chess boards and other entertainments. Justin approaches the bar to ask about lodgings. As he approaches the bar, we hear an eerie female song. We notice there are no bards in the bar. The regulars shush people as the song echoes through the tavern.

After the song finishes, the bar regulars resume their chat. A human approaches with two long-stemmed glasses of what turns out to be elvish wine to the two elves in our party (Barrisso and Karthis). Karthis thanks him. He introduces himself as **Alan** the proprietor. We ask about lodgings; with all the refugees, that is tricky, he says. He asks us to sit down at the bar.

"I've got a tenant upstairs who has fallen behind on her rent. Maybe you can do me a favour, and then there'll be a room available for you. Her name is **Oshalla**. An odd sort. Odder than your feathered friend here. Word is she's from a undersea kingdom. Has lots of nautical trinkets."

"Is she Triton?"

"No, Sahuagin."

We offer to help. He offers us the master key to open all doors.

---

We go to the first floor where Oshalla's room is. Up here, everyone is gambling at tables. Gralok informs everyone that we are on Flaming Fist business here. No one seems particularly bothered.

Norgreath says through the door that we just want a friendly chat. There's just silence.

Gralok asks one of the gamblers how long he's been there. "A few hours". Has there been anyone in the room? "Yeah, rumour she won't even let the cleaners in."

We decide to unlock the door and enter. We find a room which has not been cleaned in some time, full of trinkets. Oshalla rises from the bathtub, which from the smell is full of stagnant fetid water. She reaches for a trident leaning against the wall. She shouts "How did you get in here!??".

"What are you doing in our room?" bluffs Barrisso. "We are now the official renters of this room."

Meanwhile, Gralok decides to grapple her and drag her out of the bathtub.

Oshalla gets a hand free and releases two bolts of energy at Gralok, which both miss. With his Arcana knowledge, Lunghiss recognises the spell as Guiding Bolt.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast and does 7 HP of damage. She yelps in an unknown language.

Galen casts Guiding Bolt, but misses.

Lunghiss does a sneak attack with his rapier, doing 15 HP of damage. The sahuagin is looking peaky.

Karthis, to subdue her, tries to hit her with his quarterstaff, but misses.

Barrisso attacks with his rapier, but misses. His offhand attack with a scimitar hits for 6 HP.

Gralok attempts to shove her into a prone position.

Oshalla casts a spell on Gralok and Norgreath. Norgreath is paralysed. She then stands up.

Galen refrains from casting a spell in order to subdue her. Instead he attacks her with his mace, doing 3 HP.

Lunghiss attempts a sneak attack but misses.

Karthis tries to hit her with his quarterstaff again, and again misses.

Barrisso finally manages to hit her with his rapier for 10 HP, and knocks her out.

---

We tie her up and bring her down to the landlord. We check out her room, just in case. (We did save her life after all. In a fashion.) We open a drawer and find a sharkskin pouch containing some coinage - 66 GP and 49 SP. In a small wooden chest (which has its opening edged by shark teeth and is lined with red velvety material), we find a coral sculpture of something like a sahuagin but more shark-like, with red pearls for eyes. In Lunghiss' travels, he has heard of the sahuagins. He thinks this statue is of their deity, and that perhaps Oshalla is a cleric. We wonder why someone with some wealth was desperate to stay in a room without paying.

---

We return downstairs; Alan makes it clear her wants her out of the establishment. Gralok dumps her and her altar on the street. She swears, then strides away to the docks. There's a small round of applause from the tavern's patrons.

Alan says the room is ours for a week, with drinks and a meal on him.

---

A group of travellers with luggages enter the tavern and ask about rooms. Alan apologises and says it's full. As they leave, we hear murmuring about Scornubel refugees; the city was scooped from the ground.

We ask Alan for an introduction to Terina, the informant that Zodge asked us to meet. He tells us she is playing a gambling game upstairs and gives us a description. Barrisso offers to place a 1 GP bet on her roll. Only her and another gambler are left in the game - she wins, and gives Barrisso a gold piece. We ask her to go for a chat, mentioning we have been sent to meet her by Zodge.

She tells us about a tannery called the Poisoned Poseidon, which is actually a beached ship. She thinks it's a hideout for the Dead Three Cult. [Next time, we should investigate....](2023-11-15.md)
