# 1 Nov 2023

We stand aghast at the sight of the disappearance of Elturel. There is a moderately large number of people screaming and
crying fleeing the scene, less than we would have hoped unfortunately. A grey haired woman dressed in disheveled finery and
of noble bearing rides a well tacked horse leads a group of people towards the west towards Baldur’s Gate. She spots us
approaching also on horseback. She cries to us for help. She introduces herself as **Lady Sarafina Harrowdale**. She tells of the black
lightning and the demise of the city. Some people managed to flee the town before it disappeared. She asks us to accompany
the refugees to Baldur’s Gate.

We ask her about the fall of Elturel. She describes the light of the companion going out. This was followed by black lightning
stringing the ground and the companion. Then there was a sudden “umph” and the city was gone.

We discuss next steps with Lady Sarafina. We consider striking camp now for the refugees, arming the most able bodied with
the cultists scimitars, while we go back to Elturel to investigate the site where once the city stood.

A guy called **Cedric**, an ex watchman from Elturel, steps up and volunteers himself as a camp guard. He mentions an
abandoned outpost called Thirnbridge which is nearby where a camp can be established. Cedric suggests we accompany the
refugees to the outpost before we head to Elturel.

We get to the outpost is due course. A rag tag bunch of refugee straggle into a settlement of broken down walls and a small
tower. It is nevertheless defensible. It is surrounded by thick brambles affording but a single entry. There are some refugees
there already. Rumours abound of orcs and cultists, and other relations fears. People are very unsure that the Companion that
had protected them for so many years had been taken down and that the city was lost. There is talk of the gods having
abandoned them.

One of the refugees mentions a vision of a dark citadel with chains hanging off it, hanging over the city, just before the city
disappeared. This triggers a thought about the Zhentarim’s dark stronghold but we do not feel the Zhentarim had anything to
do with this.

We leave the refugees in Cedric’s hands and we head to where Elturel on e was to find out what we can.
We find a large saucer shaped depression where once the city once stood. Nothing remains except many a few jetties. Water
is trickling into the crater from the water table. We return to the encampment.

More refugees have arrived. There is now perhaps over 150 people in the encampment. A few fires have been lit and people
are gathering around. We hear from **Branik Stonefoot**, the dwarf smith, that he came across come ceremonial robes that have
been discarded. Some strange glyphs have been carved into the nearby trees, these reminded him of the dead three: Baal,
Bane and Myrkul. He reckons this place was used by cultists within the last couple of weeks. He draws us closer and whispers
that he fears some might be here now.

Galen investigates the carvings. He concludes that the marks were made some time ago and are not recent. We inform the
dwarf that these symbols are not recent and that he need not feel worried by the these marks.
We camp down for a long rest and plan for the trip in the morning to Baldur’s Gate.
The night brings a lot of fear because most of the refugees have never seen night.

In the morning we head west staying slightly north of the river where the ground is more sound. A few minor inconveniences
occur along the way but steady progress is made nonetheless. As we progress, we see trail signs of previous refugee passage.
Some small groups we overtake and they join our caravan. Some groups catch us up and they too join us. Foraging provides
sufficient sustenance for the journey.

About four days into the journey we spot a red sailed vessel going up river. We hail the boat and chat about their destination.
They are headed for Elturel. We inform them of the demise of Elturel and ask that he bring some of the refugees on to Baldur’s
Gate. They can take twenty. He makes comment that when he looks towards Elturel, he said that they see a glow where Elturel
once stood. He likens it to the light of Selune, aka the aurora.

We put the twenty selected refugees onto the boat. This group includes the least able for the road trip plus their careers or
immediate family.

We continue on the road to Baldur’s Gate. We hail a few more boats along the way. These have fishing nets. They have heard
about the refugees and volunteered to sail upriver to help. They take about ten refugees each. In the meantime, another
hundred refugees join our caravan.

After about nine days into our journey a group of riders come from the west. They are riding hard towards the east. They have
tabards of the Hellriders. We hail the riders. They are reluctant to stop. Their leader sees Barrisso’s paladinic symbol and
stops. He is reluctant to talk to us but the Lady Sarafina steps up and she asks that the Hellriders aid us in safe passage to
Baldur’s Gate. The captain of the Hellriders says, “my lady, we have more urgent matters to attend to and must leave you in
the good care of these recruits”. With that, she wheels and heads off.

We continue on towards Baldur’s Gate. At night we do see some glow from where Elturel once was. It does look like Selune’s
light. It does seem odd, however.

A few days later, about three days out of Baldur’s Gate, we see a platoon of soldiers marching towards us. They are fifty and all
are of the Flaming Fist. They are led by **Manip Lorn**. We recall that the leader of the Fist, **Duke Ravengard**, was in Elturel when
it disappeared.

Manip Lorn demands to see the leader of our group. We direct him towards Lady Serafina. He says. “what do you know about
grand duke Ravengard”? Serafina says she know nothing. Eventually she manages to persuade him that. This group are
merely refugees fleeing a cataclysmic event. She asks him to escort us to our destination.

Our caravan grows as we get closer to Baldur’s Gate. Rumours of up ahead reach us about the Fist not allowing refugees enter
the city; that Hellriders are being arrested and such. It appears that Hellriders were stirring up trouble in Baldur’s Gate.

At the outskirts of Baldur’s Gate, we are welcomed. The whole of the city reeks of blood, crime and opportunity. The flood of
refugees has grown dramatically. Lots of people are saying that Baldur’s Gate may be next. The Patriars here are all Dukes.
The leader of the Flaming Fist is the Grand Duke Ravengard and he, it seems, has fallen with Elturel. The Flaming Fist have
closed the city and no one is allowed in or out. The refugees and those living in the foulburg are up in arms over this as are the
inhabitants of the city who wish to freely leave.
The refugee camp outside the walls are running short on supplies.

We approach the gates and seek to negotiate entrance. A group of Flaming Fist are cordoned around the shut gate. It is called
the Basilisk Gate. One of the Flaming Fist calls out to us and asks us what we are doing. We seek refuge for the refugees and
good will from the Fist. We are directed to The Flame. We beseech him for aid for the refugees. Galen offers our service in
trade. The Flame, **Zodge**, agrees to hire us on as deputy Fists in trade for allowing the refugees into the city. He asks us to investigate a
series of murders in the city. He suspects that it is the work of the Dead Three.
We sign a contract of employment with the Flaming Fist to perform this investigation. It lasts until the killings have stopped for
ten days, at which point we will be awarded the reward of 200gp each in addition to his gratitude. It comes with a small salary
plus standard equipment at a 20% discount.
We have license to kill the Cultists of the Dead Three on sight and not to worry about collateral damage. [We are to seek out his informant
**Terina** at the Elfsong Tavern, and for the love of all that is holy be nice to her.](2023-11-08.md)
