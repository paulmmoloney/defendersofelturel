# 13 Dec 2023

[**Previously**](2023-12-06.md): At the Poisoned Poseidon ship-cum-tannery, we had found details of what seems to be an assassination list, with a Shohreh Netitia being the next target. During the night, we had been attacked in our room at the Elfsong Tavern. When the next day we investigate Cuiric’s Boarding House, we find that Shohreh is missing the past two days and that her room has been ransacked. It looks like there was a struggle and that she has been abducted.


---


We return to the Poisoned Poseidon tannery to ostensibly deal with the Master of Souls. On the deck we encounter four well armoured dudes. They are accompanied by a dude wielding an iron clad skull flail.

Gralok rages and launches himself at the flail wielding dude. He misses, despite being inspired.

The skull-flail wielder swings the flail at Gralok. He missed, twice. He then performs Misty Step to the south end of the deck, away from the raging barbarian.
Norgreath, attempts an Eldritch Blast and is inspired and hits doing a total of 15hp.

The first Fist of Bane attacks Karthis and does 8hp of bludgeoning damage.

The second Fist of Bane attacks Galen and does 7hp of bludgeoning damage

The third Fist of Bane moves to a better position.

Galen attacks the second Fist with his mace. He misses.

Lunghiss sneak attacks the first fist and does 16hp and then disengages back to his original position.

Karthis casts Poison Spray at the Fist attacking him. The Fist saves and is unaffected.

Barrisso casts Bless on Gralok, Lunghiss and Galen. He then moves to engage the skull-flail wielder.

The Iron Consul, as these followers of Bane are known, uses his voice of command ability to command the first and second
fists to attack again. Karthis is hit for 6hp. 

Meanwhile, the Iron Consul attacks Barrisso with his spear. He hits Barrisso for 7hp of piercing damage.

---

Gralok moves to the Iron Consul and attacks with his great axe and does 5hp slashing damage.

The skull-flailed wielder casts a greenish yellowish ray from the skull’s eye sockets towards Gralok. He misses however.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast at the skull-wielder and does 12hp.

The first Fist attacks Karthis and hits for 5hp. Karthis is down.

The second first attacks Galen and hits for 7hp. Galen is down.

The third fist misses Gralok.

Galen makes his first death save.

Lunghiss attacks the first Fist and hits for 11hp and withdraws. The fist falls. Lunghiss then moves to be close to the third fist but
behind Gralok.

Karthis makes his first death save.

Barrisso hits the skull-flail wielder with his rapier and also with his off-hand scimitar doing a total of 11hp. The skull-flail wielder
falls.

The Iron Consul calls out to his Fists and the one next to Gralok attacks the barbarian and does 4hp. The Iron Consul then
spears Barrisso for 7hp.

---

Gralok attacks the Iron Consul and hits him for 4hp.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast at the second Fist. He hits for 15hp force damage. He then moves away.

The second fist follows Norgreath, swings but misses.

The third first misses Gralok.

Galen makes his second death save, helps by the bless.

Lunghiss sneak attacks the second fist and fells him. He then withdraws.

Karthis fails his death save.

Barrisso swings both weapons at the Iron Consul and misses with both.

The Iron Consul commands the third fist to attack Gralok. The fist hits for 3hp. 

The Iron Consul misses Barrisso with his spear.

---

Gralok attacks the Iron Consul and does 13hp.

Norgreath moves to Galen and gives him a potion of healing. Galen is restored to 3hp. Galen gains another level of
exhaustion.

The third fist attacks Gralok and again misses.

He casts Cure Wounds on Karthis. Karthis regains 4hp and one level of exhaustion.

Lunghiss sneak attacks the third fist. He misses.

Karthis stands up and casts Witch Bolt at the Iron Consul. Inspiration is required however for the bolt to hit and it does 9hp.

Barrisso attacks the Iron Consul with two weapons. Inspiration means the rapier hits for 5hp.

The Iron Consul does 2hp to Gralok. He misses Barrisso with the spear.

Gralok attacks the Iron Consul and kills him. His rage means he gets an opportunity to also swing at the third fist but he
misses.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast at the third fist and does 8hp: the first damage it suffered this combat.

The third fist attacks Gralok but missed.

Galen casts Sacred Flame at the third fist. The fist takes 5hp radiant damage.

Lunghiss attempts a sneak attack but fumbles. He dual wields his silver dagger and with his off hand, he does enough to end
the fist.

---

Galen casts Cure Wounds on himself and is restored to 13hp. A second casting gives 10hp to Karthis.

Barrisso uses his Lay on Hands ability to recover 5hp.

We search the bodies. We find 4 suits of chain mail. We collect 3 maces, a spear, and an iron plated skull-flail. We also collect
25gp.

We quickly determine that the other rooms on this deck are unoccupied. We descend to the mid deck. It is unoccupied.

We descend the stairs on to a level that is below street level and has been carved from the city’s bedrock. The room’s shape
matches the deck shape of a boat.
Two fists stand either side of a set of double doors.

Karthis closes range and casts Witch Bolt at the northernmost fist. He misses.

Norgreath attacks the northernmost fist with an Eldritch Blast and does 11hp of force damage.

Gralok closes range to the southernmost fist and flings a javelin. He hits the southernmost fist for 8hp.

Galen moves closer and casts sacred flame at the northernmost fist. The fist saves however.

The double doors open and a skull-flail wielder emerges. Unlike the previous skull-flail wielders, this guy’s flail is coated in
silver. He holds up his flail and a bolt streams out of it. The bolt blossoms into a huge explosion centred on the party. Everyone
in the party are engulfed in the explosion. Those that fail their dexterity save take 23hp of necrotic damage. Those
that succeed take 11hp. The upshot is that everyone is down except Gralok.

Galen succeeds at his first death save.

Barrisso succeeds on his fist death save.

The southernmost fist attacks Gralok with his mace. He hits Gralok for 5hp.

The northernmost fist attacks Gralok with his mace and downs Gralok.

We are all down.

---

We all awake, our wounds crudely treated. We all have a single hitpoint. All of our weapons are gone. We are incarcerated in
locked cells two to a cell. Rousing ourselves we hear banging on the door. The door is a strong all-metal affair. Lunghiss tries to
pick the lock and succeeds. He opens the door and steps outside. There he sees some Flaming Fist dudes. Apparently Flame
Zodge got our message and came to help. Lunghiss unlocks all the party’s cells. 

The chamber we enter into is a long corridor with alcoves on the north and south walls. In many of these we mostly see frames for tanning. In one alcove, however, there is
a pile of stuff apparently taken of the cult’s victims. There we find our weapons but not our gold. We also find a bunch of
refugee permits. Karthis recognises that there is a remarkable similarity between them. He thinks these may be forgeries.
Perhaps a check on central records would prove this for sure.

We move into the next chamber where we find an operating table. On it is a corpse of a human female. A table beside carries a
pile of surgical implements. A brazier holds three branding irons. There is an iron to brand the symbol of each of the Dead
Three. There are lots of alcoves on the wall with scrolls within.

We confer with Zodge. The corpse is, in fact, that of Shohreh Netitia. We share with him all we have learned so far.
He asks us if we are actually Hellriders. He indicates that they have found a list in this ship/tannery of all known Hellriders.
Zodge points to the entries for each of our names. He says that this indicates to him why we have not been killed by the cult. 
He points to Shohreh and says that she and the other murder victims are related to or otherwise significantly connects with a
Hellrider. Zodge goes on to says that, in fact, we have not been killed just now because the cult sees us as sacrifices to their
dread divinities.

He asks us what our next actions would be. We indicate the need for rest and thereafter an investigation of the bathhouse. He
reckons that we have a vested interest in discovering what’s what, seeing as how we’re on their list.
We notice the dead about us does not include a corpse for the skull-flail wielder. Zodge tells us that the Master of Souls, in the end, disappeared and made his escape as darkness descended on the Flaming Fist vanguard. The Master of Souls gave the
Flaming Fist the Flaming Slip, it appears.

Zodge give us 5GP each to tide us over. He suggests we go back to the Elfsong Tavern and rest up. He asks us to get back in the fight as
soon as we can before the bodies start to pile up. He gives us one final piece of paper: a message to the Master of Souls from someone named Vaaz:

???+ scroll "To Master of Souls from Vaaz"

	Master of Souls,
	
	I hope all things are well. I have heard
	complaints from one of my Fists, but I
	assure you that neither I nor the Emissary 
	lend it any credence. The Emissary does 
	suggest, however, that your minions begin
	dumping the bodies farther afield. He 
	wants us to "spread the terror" and is 
	also concerned that the park may become 
	a security concern.

	Vaaz

We return to the Elfsong and [take a long rest](../2024/2024-01-03.md).

**We all level up. We also lose one level of exhaustion.**
