# 22 July 2026

**[Previously](2026-07-16.md):** We managed to commandeer the Tyrant of War and convinced our new companion Ixona not to use it on innocents in order to kill Baron Thutin, who murdered her family back on her world of K'thar. We attempted to destroy the vehicle in the Positive Energy Plane - but it seems even its constant explosion of energy was not enough to destroy it. But we deemed it to be out of reach of those who might misuse it, so have travelled with Ixona to her homeworld. We teleported near to the baron's heavily-defended castle and sent Taq, Norgreath's familiar, in to reconnoitre, the party hitching a ride in Norgreath's ring and sequestering ourselves in a bathroom. Taq mapped out much of the castle, including lavish bedrooms which seem to belong to the baron's seven knight guardians. He also found a bedroom containing a two-handed sword, and a hexagonal table with a devilish symbol - this perhaps is the baron's. The last room that Taq found is seemingly a war room - where we see the Knight of Stars, one of the baron's guardians and the one that Ixona hates the most. She shouted to us that she wanted to immediately kill him...

---

We hold our breaths as someone playing the harpsichord in the next room may have heard Ixona's shout. But he keeps playing. We plan a course of action: perhaps use Taq to create a distraction by setting fire to the straw roof of a building across the courtyard from where we are. But we decide we will first despatch the two people in the drawing room outside our bathroom.

Lunghiss casts Mage Hand so he can give himself advantage on attacks. Taq moves to a position, in the guise of a spider, where he can alert us if defenders come towards the area we're in.

Gralok holds an action to burst out once someone Hastes him, while using Call the Hunt.

Karthis casts Haste on Gralok. Gralok runs out and attacks the person to the south at the harpsichord. He hits, doing 36HP of damage.

Lunghiss moves and attacks the same guy: piano man is dead. Fortunately, he does not make a noise as he collapsed on his keyboard.

Ixona rushes from the bathroom. So does Norgreath, heading towards the guy at the north end of the room. He casts  Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast and Genie's Wrath), doing 32HP of damage; his target cries out in pain.

Barrisso darts towards the same guy and attacks with his Fool's Shortsword: his first hit does 25HP of damage. He goes slack and his book falls to the floor. Barrisso catches a glimpse of the book: it's about animal husbandry.

Most of the party move into position behind double doors beyond which are a host of defenders in an east-west corridor. Ixona takes her strange eye out and readies it. Norgreath moves into position, hovering five feet off the ground.

Galen casts Holy Aura on the entire party.

Gralok then opens the double doors.

Karthis casts Lightning Bolt down the corridor at the enemy. He managed to get all but one of them in the beam. All are frazzled but none fall.

Gralok moves into the corridor and attacks a guard, felling him. He gets to attack the next guard, killing the next one as well. He continues on, killing a third, and manages to wound a fourth.

Lunghiss moves and attacks a target, killing him.

An archer takes a shot at Lunghiss but misses.

Double doors on the south side of the corridor open. A full-helmed knight in plate mail with a heavy longsword bursts in, calling out "It is I! Sir Connadus! Prepare to face your doom!".

Ixona sends her sphere flying towards the knight, doing 40HP of damage.

Norgreath casts Chain Lightning at the knight. He takes full damage (49HP) and the bolt jumps to the nearest enemy, who dies. The next closest enemy takes another bolt of lightning, but remains on his feet. Another guard also takes a bolt and dies.

Norgreath dodges three Arcane Burst spells, while two guards run up the stairs from below. Another fires a heavy crossbow but misses.

Barrisso flies towards the knight and attacks, doing 33HP of damage, using Divine Smite. As a reaction, when the knight is struck, briars shoot out from his arms to entangle Barrisso and pull him down to the ground, but he fails. Barrisso attacks again, but misses.

A lightning bolt is cast at the party - those who save take 16HP of damage.

Another knight bursts into the corridor - this is the Star Knight. He attacks Barrisso, doing 44HP of damage.

He calls out to Sir Connadus: "I have come to aid you!".

Within a square around some of the group, black tentacles erupt from the floor *(Strength saving throw)* but the party successfully avoid their grasp.

Galen casts Command on the knights, ordering them to flee, but they appear to be immune to such magic. He then attacks the nearest with his Marble Mace, but misses.

An archer attacks Barrisso with his longbow: one arrow hits for 6HP.

Taq changes back into imp form, remaining invisible.

An assassin attacks Gralok with a shortsword: one attack slips through, doing 3HP of piercing plus 24HP of poison damage. Thanks to the Heroes' Feast spell, Gralok is immune to poison. The assassin tries to disengage, but Gralok can still hit him, doing 10HP damage.

Gralok moves out of the reach of the black tentacles and attacks the assassin, doing 69HP of damage.

An enemy mage casts Cone of Cold at some of the party: those who don't fail take 24HP of damage.

Two enemy knights retreat down the stairs, to the courtyard, screaming.

Karthis casts Psychic Scream up to 90 feet from him. Five of our enemies' heads explode, including Sir Connadus.

Another enemy mage moves from the war room to the double doors and casts Cone of Cold, doing 20HP of cold damage to the affected.

Lunghiss uses his Mage Hand to give himself advantage on a sneak attack using Lathander's Radiant Blade, on the the Star Knight, inflicting 58HP of damage. As he strikes him, the Star Knight casts Finger of Death. Lunghiss survives, but takes 27HP of necrotic damage.

Ixona throws her eye at the Star Knight, doing 53HP of damage.

Norgreath attempts to fly towards the knight but is restrained by the black tentacles, suffering 16HP of bludgeoning damage. He casts  Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast and Genie's Wrath) at the knight, doing 13HP of damage.

The Star Knight collapses, but then rises again as a shadowy form.

Norgreath attacks again, doing another 13HP of damage.

An enemy necromancer casts Circle of Death at the party. We all save, taking 12HP of necrotic damage. But it also seems to heal the Star Knight.

Barrisso utters a Vow of Enmity against the Star Knight, then attacks with his Fool's Shortsword, doing 35HP of damage. In retaliation, the Star Knight casts Finger of Death at him: Barrisso manages to take only 14HP of necrotic damage.

Barrisso uses Soul of Vengeance on the Star Knight, doing (with Divine Smite) 35HP of damage.

Barrisso's second attack *(a critical)* does 46HP of damage.

The Star Knight attacks Barrisso, but misses.

Galen casts Mass Heal, restoring the whole party to full hit points.

An enemy attacks with their longbow but misses.

Gralok attacks the knight with his Blazing Cold Greataxe, doing . And again, in retaliation, the knight casts Finger of Death, inflicting 28HP of damage. On Gralok's final attack, the knight disappears into a mist. Gralok rushes into the war room to attack the Baron but misses.

Karthis casts Fireball to the end of the corridor between two opponents: both die in the conflagration.

The Baron seems to have disappears and moved somewhere.

Lunghiss moves into the war room. Taq tells Norgreath that he saw the Baron's archmage join him on a balcony, and then both disappeared. He's pretty sure that they used a Teleport spell...
