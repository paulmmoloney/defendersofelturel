# 16 July 2026

**[Previously](2026-07-16.md):** We managed to commandeer the Tyrant of War and convinced our new companion Ixona not to use it on innocents in order to kill Baron Thutin, who murdered her family back on her world of K'thar. We attempted to destroy the vehicle in the Positive Energy Plane - but it seems even its constant explosion of energy was not enough to destroy it. But we deemed it to be out of reach of those who might misuse it, so have travelled with Ixona to her homeworld. We teleported near to the baron's heavily-defended castle and sent Taq, Norgreath's familiar, in to reconnoitre. Taq mapped out much of the castle, including lavish bedrooms which seem to belong to the baron's seven knight guardians. He also found a bedroom containing a two-handed sword, and a hexagonal table with a devilish symbol - this perhaps is the baron's. The last room that Taq found is seemingly a war room - where we see the Knight of Stars, one of the baron's guardians and the one that Ixona hates the most. She shouted to us that she wanted to immediately kill him...
