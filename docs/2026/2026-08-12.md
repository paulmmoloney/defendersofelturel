# 12 Aug 2026

**[Previously](2026-08-05.md):** We managed to commandeer the Tyrant of War and convinced Ixona not to use it on innocents in order to kill Baron Thutin, who had murdered her family back on her world of K'thar. We travelled to K'thar and fought the baron's allies. While the Baron managed to escape along with his archmage, we cleared the inner keep of his men and freed Edmund, the heir of the previous baron, from the castle keep. We methodically cleared out the inner keep, with Barrisso succeeding in getting guards in the main courtyard to surrender. When we reached the outer keep, we found that was where most of the usurper's forces had gathered. Barrisso got one group of guards to move away to the main gate. At that point, Karthis attacked them with two Fireballs, and now all the baron's forces are raining down physical and magical attacks on us.
