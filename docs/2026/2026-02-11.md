# 11 Feb 2026

**[Previously](2026-02-04.md):** The party returned to the valley, where the hunted magic users are making their last stand, to aid them in the assault against them by Tycho and his army. We killed Tycho and repelled his army's attack on the keep. On the body of Isolde, Tycho's champion, we found a diary, with Tycho telling Isolde: *"The book told Daeral of the Heaun the method of crafting the Dragon Doom"*. This seems to refer to the sentient book [we met earlier]. In the keep, we secretly consulted with Maon (the leader of the spellcasters, who we previously met consulting with the sentient book) and Real (the last dragon). Daeral of the Heaun is a figure from ancient history. The Heaun were a tribe of magic-users that lived far to the east, across the sea. Daeral was a great mage of their tribe. Maon remembers that Daeral fought a dragon in the past. Our next step is to consult the sentient book....

---

Maon rests the book upon its great stand and opens it. It manifests its face from one of the pages.

We ask the book:

"What do you know of a person called Daeral of the Heaun?"

The face scowls.

"What about him?"

"We heard that you have had deals with him in the past."

"500 years ago!"

"He requested certain capabilities."

"That's what people ask of me."

"In this case, dragons, and how to create the Dragon Doom. Did you or Daeral create the Dragon Doom? We want to determine the best way to destroy it. If you created it, you can help us uncreate it."

"Neither of us created it. It is a shard of the petrified Heart of the Giant. It was created in the hills.

"How do we destroy it?"

"Make it one with the rest of the Heart of the Giant."

"Real's father was banished most recently - can we get him back if the Dragon Doom is destroyed?"

"Banishment means being sent to a random plane of existence. He would have the ability to return if he can return. But are you sure bringing back the dragons is a good idea?"

"We believe the presence of dragons is tied to the ability to use magic. That is up to you who live here to decide."

We ask about the Heart of the Giant.

"The giant's petrified corpse is in a cavern in the hills, frozen in his final act of saving the world from an immense eruption."

We turn to ask Meon her opinion. She looks horrified. We ask her to go outside and ask her what she thinks.

"I... I don't know if we can trust the Book. It has never told us about being in any way involved with the Dragon Doom. Daeral of the Heaun wrote the credo of the Unity. They persecuted us for 500 years! I would be reluctant to bring dragonkind back to our world."

We ask her about the lore surrounding these events and people.

"The degenerate giant kin you have met are the result of the strife between giants and dragons: dragons won that war aeons ago. The petrified giant, named Xeluan, is a creature of legend, considered to be the saviour of the world, martyred holding back a geological catastrophe. He stopped eruptions and earthquakes by holding together the earth: later he was found and venerated, and a constant watch is held over his remains by humans."

"Will they resist us if we return with the Dragon Doom?"

"There has been a trade in shards from Xeluan for many decades. The guards are there to stop that trade. Once the Heart of the Giant was broken, some of the corpse's integrity collapsed."

"Where have the shards ended up?"

"All over the world it seems. Some of them retain their magic. But they do not seem to be as powerful as the Dragon Doom."

---

Before anything else, we decide to return to the city in order to retrieve Gralok and Barrisso's gear which they lost during their execution.  

Taq retrieves the breastplate holding the amulet. Norgreath casts Demiplane in order to have somewhere to safely stash the Dragon Doom, creating a stone door through which there is a 30 cubic feet room. Inside, the room is empty. Norgreath tosses a single copper coin into one of its coins to identify it. He steps away to allow Taq to fly in.

As Taq approaches the doorway, it disappears. Taq flies backwards, and the doorway reappears. It seems that stashing the Dragon Doom in a demiplane is not going to work.

---

We travel back to the city by entering Norgreath's magic ring, which Barrisso carries. We enter the city under the cover of darkness. Barrisso wears the Hat of Disguise to blend in with the locals. Although it does not disguise his wings, he wears a black cloak to hide them. We enter our old hideout and leave the ring. Taq stashes the Dragon Doom somewhere safe but not near where we may exit the building.

The general mood of the city seems to be more militaristic than our last visit. Regular patrols are occurring, and more militia are on the streets. 

Galen casts Locate Object on Gralok's Raggadragga's Maul, and senses it is in the fortress where Gralok and Barrisso were executed. Hopefully all their equipment is near to that.

---

Gralok and Karthis stay behind in the hideout to guard the Dragon Doom and to examine the spellbooks we captured earlier. The rest of us re-enter the ring, and Taq carries us to the fortress, entering at the level where we think Gralok's and Barrisso's belongings are. Taq uses his spider ability to enter a dungeon at the bottom of the fortress where he finds several chests.  He confirms there is no one else in the room, and we leave the ring. We made the doors of the room safe using spikes. Norgreath takes out our Bag of Holding. We see four chests, which Lunghiss checks for traps and then unlocks. We see all our old equipment. Additionally, in the fourth chest, there is 2000GP and 10 x 200GP gems. We either re-equip items or put them into the Bag of Holding.

Norgreath casts Seeming so that we all look like regular militia. He will also use Ghostly Gaze in order to scan ahead and try to ensure we do not encounter anyone who might discover us. We make our way *(Deception)*. Lunghiss casts Invisibility on himself so that the others can instead use Persuasion to sweet talk their way out of the building while he follows them.

They successfully leave the building and head over the bridge, and head back to the hideout. Gralok and Karthis are still there and safe, along with the Dragon Doom. Gralok and Barrisso now have all their equipment back. 

In the meantime, Karthis had time to copy a Teleport spell from the spellbooks we found. 

---

We return in the ring to the keep. We prepare for our trip to the giant's tomb, and [ask Maon to accompany us.](2026-02-18.md)
