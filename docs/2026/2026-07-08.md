# 8 July 2026

**[Previously](2026-07-01.md):** The reformed key for the Tyrant of War created a portal leading us that vehicle. There, we fought and killed Captain Zareta, leader of the Golden Skulls pirates. Later on her body, we recovered an agreement between her and a devilish person, promising a tithe of souls from her use of the Tyrant of War in exchange for safe passage of the ship through the lower plains, and a magical compass. We restrained Zareta's crew but, as Barrisso imprisoned them in a room on the ship, nine devils materialised and slaughtered them. On defeating the last of these devils, one shouted "Scribe! Now it is time" and another devil appeared: this one holding a scroll, whose body was covered with unintelligible writing, and whose tail dripped a red liquid - blood or ink? Lunghiss and Barrisso killed her. Norgreath found Ixona in the craft's control room, who explained she wanted to use the craft's full force to kill a baron on her plain who killed hundreds, including her family. But she does not seem to care if she will herself kill many innocents. Barrisso convinces her to rethink her plan, and instead she agrees to our promise to kill this baron ourselves. Now we just need to navigate the craft back to her plain.
