# 8 July 2026

**[Previously](2026-07-01.md):** The reformed key for the Tyrant of War created a portal leading us that vehicle. There, we fought and killed Captain Zareta, leader of the Golden Skulls pirates. Later on her body, we recovered an agreement between her and a devilish person, promising a tithe of souls from her use of the Tyrant of War in exchange for safe passage of the ship through the lower plains, and a magical compass. We restrained Zareta's crew but, as Barrisso imprisoned them in a room on the ship, nine devils materialised and slaughtered them. On defeating the last of these devils, one shouted "Scribe! Now it is time" and another devil appeared: this one holding a scroll, whose body was covered with unintelligible writing, and whose tail dripped a red liquid - blood or ink? Lunghiss and Barrisso killed her. Norgreath found Ixona in the craft's control room, who explained she wanted to use the craft's full force to kill a baron on her plain who had killed hundreds, including her family. But she did not seem to care if she would herself kill many innocents. Barrisso convinced her to rethink her plan, and instead she accepted our promise to kill this baron ourselves. Now we just need to navigate the craft back to her plane.

---

Suddenly, an appendage from the floor casts a ball of green fire at Barrisso but misses.

"Ixona, how do we navigate this craft?"

"I don't know. We need to someone to figure out how to use this cauldron using a map of nearby dimensions."

We recall a map we found in Captain Zareta's belongings. cosmology of the known Hells

Barrisso attempts to attune with the cauldron. He sees, forming in the fog, a stylised image of a plane and nodes connected by lines. There are labels in Infernal attached to those nodes and lines. But the image dissipates.

We ask Ixona where she is from: she says her world is called K'thar.

Karthis attempts to attune to the navigation cauldron, and sees similar images. He seems the Tyrant of War itself hanging in one of the dimensions. But similar to Barrisso, the image dissipates.

Lunghiss also tries, but only sees as much as Karthis did.

Gralok tries, but looks as if he trying to drink from the cauldron. We pull him back.

Norgreath also tries to figure out this cauldron. But again, the same result.

Ixona tries; this time, she is able to manipulate the image in the fog. She zooms into the image to show the Tyrant of War, then zooms out again, muttering as she tries to find a path across the cosmos to our destination.

Karthis *(Arcana check)* notices the links do not correspond with the Great Wheel cosmology that he is familiar with. Norgreath, who is the most familiar with the other method of navigating the planes, the path of the Planebreaker, realises that this representation doesn't fit with that either. The Planebreaker moves from world to world, plane to plane in a seemingly haphazard way, and Norgreath's token allows him to follow along in its wake. However, the cauldron conjures connections that the Planebreaker has not forged. What seems strange to our party is that this is a cosmology which does not fit with our previous notions. From our point of view, it is a random series of connections between places and we perhaps just have to accept it.

Eventually, Ixona finds a region labelled Positive Energy Plane and locks that in on the navigation.

"Our path is set. If we go to the command deck, we can travel to our destination."

---

We leave the navigation room and go back to the room where we found Captain Zareta. As Barrisso tries to sit in the emerald  chair, devilish limbs reach out to grab him and he is now restrained.

Karthis casts Fire Bolt at one of those limbs. He hits, but is has no affect.

Karthis does a critical hit on a limb. But despite that, he still does no damage. This limb could be metaphysical.

Gralok tries to grapple a limb and gets a good hold on it.

Barrisso tries to free himself: he manages not to be pulled into the deck, but is still grappled.

Norgreath distracts a limb with Mage Hand.

Galen casts Bless on Barrisso, Lunghiss, and Norgreath. He then hits one of the limbs with his Marble Mace, expending all its force. The limb contracts into the floor.

Ixona sits in the other, crimson, throne in the room. She pulls some levers and the view outside the craft through a greenish veil shifts along the path revealed in the navigation cauldron. We all feel a deep bone-rattling rumbling and a portal appears in front of the craft.

Karthis casts Ray of Frost at one of the limb: it hits it squarely, but has no effect.

Lunghiss casts Mage Hand at one of the limbs, hoping to distract it.

Barrisso attempts to Misty Step to where Lunghiss is standing, but cannot move. He attempts to resist the limbs once more and the limbs withdraw.

"What does Barrisso need to do to move the craft?"

"Don't do anything!", says Ixona.

Barrisso seems to be sitting in the local navigation chair rather than the inter-plane navigation chair.

In the meantime, the portal in front of the craft has grown big enough for the Tyrant of War to pass through. The craft moves through it and jumps instantly to the Positive Energy plane. When we arrive at our destination, we are exposed to a blinding light *(Constitution save)*. Those who affected are blinded. Those not affected still have to cover their eyes.

We also find *(Constitution save)* there is no air and we have to hold our breaths. Strangely, we also find ourselves getting some of our health back.

Norgreath shouts "Plane shift!" and the rest of the party notice, grabbing each others hands to form a circle. He initiates a plane shift back to our teleportation circle back on Elturel.

We realise if we had stayed on the Positive Energy plane for any while longer, we would have exploded. We are hoping that the Tyrant of War we left behind has been destroyed. We consider confirming that once we have rested.

The party takes a long rest.

**Everyone goes up to Level 17.**

--

After our rest, we notice that Ixona is amazed by our abode. "Everything is ... so peaceful here."

She asks Karthis if she can look at his spellbooks and he agrees.

Some of the party are still blind after their exposure to the Positive Energy plane, which Galen tries to cure with Lesser Restoration. However, that does not work: their condition is permanent not temporary blindness, so it needs Greater Restoration. We take time to bring Ixona to see the sights of Elturel.

---

We then try to use the key for the Tyrant of War to see if it still exists or has been destroyed. A portal appears: through the portal, we find that the craft has not been destroyed. We decide that while not destroyed, it's still out of commission and we will check it against after we aid Ixona in her quest to destroy the baron who killed her family...
