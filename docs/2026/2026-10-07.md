# 7 Oct 2026

**[Previously](2026-09-30.md):** During a gruelling battle with the ex-Baron Thutin, now converted along with his minions into a creature from the Void, four of our party were felled and we retreated, without Galen, back to Elturel. After two long rests, Norgreath used his magic coin to bring us back to K'thar. There, Taq scouted ahead and found that the baronial castle has already been assaulted by these Void creatures, with broken gateways and walls and bodies strewn everywhere. Norgreath tried to locate Void Thutin using Scrying, but attacked psychically. We observed that the sky on K'thar is now a kaleidoscope of impossible colours. As we travelled to the Court of the Lunar Knight we encountered Void-twisted remnants: trees with human eyes, animals merged into chimeras. The bottom of the Court has vanished, and only the remains of a tower floating fifty feet off the ground remain. A rift above the tower is syphoning off material into the void, and our friend Galen hovers, seemingly neither alive nor dead, under it. The party found that when we attempted to rescue him, we are instantly teleported back to the floor of the tower. Norgreath used Thunder Step to get to Galen, only to be attacked by several tendrils that seem to belong to Thutin; these also grasp Galen, making it impossible to move him. Eventually, we destroyed all four tentacles; Norgreath used that chance to grab Galen and Plane Shift away. Now the rest of us are in a fight to the death with the Void Lord...
