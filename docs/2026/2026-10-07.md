# 7 Oct 2026

**[Previously](2026-09-30.md):** During a gruelling battle with the ex-Baron Thutin, now converted along with his minions into a creature from the Void, four of our party were felled and we retreated, without Galen, back to Elturel. After two long rests, Norgreath used his magic coin to bring us back to K'thar. There, Taq scouted ahead and found that the baronial castle has already been assaulted by these Void creatures, with broken gateways and walls and bodies strewn everywhere. Norgreath tried to locate Void Thutin using Scrying, but attacked psychically. We observed that the sky on K'thar is now a kaleidoscope of impossible colours. As we travelled to the Court of the Lunar Knight we encountered Void-twisted remnants: trees with human eyes, animals merged into chimeras. The bottom of the Court has vanished, and only the remains of a tower floating fifty feet off the ground remain. A rift above the tower is syphoning off material into the void, and our friend Galen hovers, seemingly neither alive nor dead, under it. The party found that when we attempted to rescue him, we are instantly teleported back to the floor of the tower. Norgreath used Thunder Step to get to Galen, only to be attacked by several tendrils that seem to belong to Thutin; these also grasp Galen, making it impossible to move him. Eventually, we destroyed all four tentacles; Norgreath used that chance to grab Galen and Plane Shift away. Now the rest of us are in a fight to the death with the Void Lord...

---

The party are left without two warlocks, who were the escape plan with Plane Shift. Norgreath disappeared with Galen, presumably using Plane Shift. His simulacrum was attacked by the tentacle monster from the void, and dropped in a single round. Taq is also down. Gralok is feasting on his fellow party members. So the remaining characters need to decide what to do.

Gralok moves next, attacking Lunghiss. He is still hasted. He hits multiple times for 35HP damage, and requires a CON save DC14 three times, to avoid extra necrotic damage. He fails twice, taking 5HP in extra damage.

Karthis removes concentration on his Haste spell on Gralok.

Lunghiss casts Invisibility on himself, then casts Mage Hand as a bonus, then moves to the north-west, hopefully out of range of Gralok's teeth.

Karthis shouts to everyone to gather around him: he plans to Teleport everyone out of there. In the meantime, he casts Power Word Stun on the Void Lord. Seemingly, it has no effect.

The Void Lord reaches out with a tendril towards Lunghiss. It does not attack him; rather, it pulls the Deck of Many Things from his backpack, which hovers in front of them, and takes a card named "The Pit". The Void spiral around us grows and expands to where Lunghiss is standing. The card itself disintegrates, but the rest of the pack remains.

---

Back on Elturel, Norgreath has arrived back at his sanctuary with the broken and damaged body of Galen. Norgreath casts Resurrection. Galen is restored, but is missing many of his items (such as his Marble Mace) and his armour is damaged (-2 AC).

---

Ixona takes something out of her pouch that looks like a hefty coin. She says "Should we shift?" She uses a Misty Step to move beside Karthis. But nothing *(Arcana fail)* happens.

The Void Lord attacks Barrisso with his tendrils, injuring him badly.

Barrisso retaliates with his Fool's Shortsword, doing 42HP using Divine Smite. A second attack does 22HP. His offhand attack connects as well.

Gralok, after losing the effect of Karthis' Haste, is too weak to move.

Another card raises from the Deck of Many Things: "Tavern". The card starts to shred, but this one makes everyone in the room seem more impressive than they were before. *(For the next two rounds, everyone gets +2 on Charisma checks.)*

Lunghiss takes a Greater Potion of Healing (12HP), moves towards Karthis, and the moves his mage hand beside the Void Lord.

Karthis moves to encompass the whole party and casts Teleport, targetting the baron's castle. We appear at the castle, intact and (for now) safe. Including the still-ravenous Gralok.

---

Back in Elturel, Galen is restored to health, but exhausted (two levels).

---

Back at the castle, Ixona looks distraught. "This world is doomed." Fortunately, at that moment, Gralok frees himself of his Insatiable Hunger curse.

Ixona asks: "Where do we want to go?" We say Elturel. She conjures up the familiar silver path. As we climb onto the path, we gain a better perspective of the wold around us. The void in the sky is growing, minute by minute. We sense nine percussions happen, where the whole of the firmament shakes. We keep walking on the path, and find ourselves in a flat area of newly-created ice and snow. We see cities encased in ice, familiar to those of us who have seen Baldur's Gate, but as if they have suddenly been captured in glaciers. We continue on.

We see another landscape that has undergone catastrophe. Everything is drowned by dark water, with the occasional tower poking from the waves. We seen massive tentacles undulating under the surface. We continue one.

The next place seems more familiar, and we find a side-path that leads us down towards Elturel and eventually we reach Norgreath's tower. Norgreath recounts what has happened to himself and Galen. We decide we need at least a few days to fully recuperate and re-armour. We realise that Ixona must be traumatised by the fact that her world is gone, and that perhaps she was the cause of that. We discuss what strategy we need to end the Void Lord's reign. Do we use the Tyrant of War? Should we meet with Manizer again? Or even the Mantis?
