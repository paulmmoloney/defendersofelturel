# 16 Sep 2026

**[Previously](2026-09-09.md):** We cleared the (now ex-baron) Thutin's castle of his forces, rescued Edmund, the son of the rightful baron. and headed to The Court of the Lunar Knight to vanquish Thutin. There we made our way to its upper level and commenced battle with him and his allies, including Lady Morgant. Karthis led with a Fire Ball that singed a group to our north-east. Lunghiss cast his Shadow Blade and took down the statue-like enemy with one blow. Lady Morgant tried to down him in retaliation with Moonbeam, but Lunghiss used Spell Thief to cancel its effect. Then an enemy mage used Maddening Darkness to obscure everyone's vision and inflict psychic damage on the party. Lunghiss badly wounded Lady Morgant, while Galen blinded Thutin and three others using Sunbeam. Thutin ordered his minions to kill Galen, who survived a knife attack. Gralok killed the enemy archmage, who at that point resurrected as a gigantic tentacle worm-like creature from the Void. Lady Ruth called out. "Heralds! The next sign of the comet has come as planned! Hold your course!", then felled Karthis with her rapier. A shadow fey duellist struck Barrisso down, while several attacks left Norgreath almost out of health. Norgreath grabbed the lifeless Karthis and used Thunder Step to escape. The damage done by this killed both Lady Morgant and a shadow fey duellist, who in turn both resurrected as Void creatures. Lunghiss sneak-attacked Thutin, while Galen used Mass Heal to heal the whole party. Barrisso attempted to use Banishment on the gigantic worm but failed. One of the Void creatures used Collapse Distance to teleport Norgreath right beside the worm, inflicting damage on Karthis who Norgreath had been aiding. Norgreath then cast Banishment on two of the Void creatures and succeeded. The battle continues...

---

Barrisso attacks Thutin with his Fool's Shortsword, but misses.

Lady Ruth moves position.

Gralok attacks Thutin with his Blazing Cold Greataxe, killing him. 

As he falls, Lady Ruth calls out: "FOOLISH MORTALS! YOU THOUGHT YOU WERE FELLING A TYRANT? YOU WERE ONLY CLEARING THE STAGE! EVERY DROP OF BLOOD THAT FALLS CLEARS THE COMET TO THIS SPHERE!".

The late ex-baron is now the largest, most gruesome Void creature we have seen so far.

Gralok follows up with an attack on Lady Ruth, but misses.

Lady Ruth casts a Flood Blast at Gralok, who *(Strength save)* succeeds in not being knocked prone. She then attacks with her longsword, doing 18HP of damage.

The Void Thutin now attacks Gralok, Barrisso, and Norgreath with his tendrils. He hits Barrisso and Norgreath, doing 27HP and 37HP bludgeoning damage respectively. Fortunately, Norgreath maintains his concentration on his Banishment spell.

Suddenly, water ripples out from around Lady Ruth and her form shimmers; we now find her harder to see.

A shadow fey dualist stabs Galen with his rapier, doing 38HP of damage and poisoning him.

Ixona is attacked by a shadow fey dualist and injured.

Another shadow fey dualist attacks Norgreath, doing 41HP of damage.

Taq stays invisible, keeping an eye out for strange things (or even stranger).

The Void Duellist points her staff at Norgreath. The space around him warps, but *(Wisdom save)* he avoids any damage.

Norgreath casts Synaptic Static at the Void Thutin and Lady Ruth, but they both avoid the full effect, taking 13HP of psychic damage.

Lady Ruth says: "This Ixona you serve is a puppet on a string. She is opening the gate to Nothingness. Check the void cards in her deck."

"Who is she a puppet of?"

"Can you not see the results of your actions around you?"

Karthis yells "Surrender or Die!", while casting Haste on Gralok.

Lunghiss sneak attacks the nearest fey duellist, killing him. Another unique Void creature emerges in his wake. As he dies, Lady Ruth cries out "We do not fall, we return! Assemble the full circuit."

As a bonus action, Galen casts Power Word Fortify, giving everyone (except Lunghiss) temporary hit points. He then attacks the nearest fey duellist with his Marble Mace, doing 44HP of damage.

---

Barrisso attempts Banishment on Void Thutin but fails. As a bonus, he casts Misty Step. He attacks Lady Ruth with his scimitar, doing 18HP of damage to her.

Gralok chugs a Potion of Hill Giant Strength, giving him 21 Strength. He attacks Lady Ruth: the first misses, the second is a critical, doing 35HP damage.

Lady Ruth retaliates, attacking with her longsword, doing 45HP of damage to Gralok. She then flies across to the other side of the Void Thutin.

The Void Thutin attacks Barrisso, Norgreath and Gralok, doing 37HP to Barrisso.

A fey duellist wounds Galen, while another attacks Norgreath, doing 9HP of damage.

The Void Duellist attacks Ixona with a Void-infused pseudopod; she is down to 15HP and her intelligence reduced by 4.

The Void Duellist uses Psychic Orb on Ixona, and she falls to the ground.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast and Genie's Wrath) on the Void Duellist, doing 20HP of damage and pushing it away through Ixona's sphere. But it does not seem to take that extra damage. He then targets a fey duellist but misses. He attacks again, this time Lady Ruth, but again misses.

Ixona succeeds her death save.

A Void creature cries "Looks on our true forms! The baron's old form was a farce!"

Karthis casts Chain Lightning at Lady Ruth, Void Thutin, and Void Duellist. Lady Ruth opens her arms and absorbs all of the lightning. She plays the lightning from the one hand to the other.

Lunghiss moves and sneak attacks the Void Duellist to the south-east with his Shadow Blade. He does 72HP of damage (plus 12HP thunder damage if they subsequently move), then moves away using Dash as a bonus action.

Galen casts Sunbeam at the fey duellist, the Void Duellist and Lady Ruth to the north; she takes only half damage. But the fey duellist dies, and there is now a Void creature in its place, with translucent skin, lidless eyes, and far too many teeth. Only one human enemy, a fry duellist, remains.

---

Barrisso moves and attacks Lady Ruth with his shortsword, but misses each time.

Gralok moves (taking an attack of opportunity which misses), then attacks Lady Ruth, doing 44HP of damage.

Lady Ruth attacks Gralok with her longsword, doing 20HP of damage.

Void Thutin attacks Barrisso, doing 37HP of damage. Another attack on him does 27HP of damage. A third attack fells Barrisso.

The translucent Void Duellist does a Slam attack, doing 29HP of bludgeoning damage.

The last fey duellist attacks Norgreath, hitting three times, doing 34HP of damage. Fortunately, he maintains concentration.

Another Void creature attacks Gralok with a Void-infused pseudopod but misses.

The Void Duellist to the south-east casts Psychic Orb at Karthis, doing 35HP of psychic damage. Norgreath is also hit by a second damage, which does 21HP of psychic damage.

Norgreath casts Simulacrum. His new doppelganger then casts Banishment on both Lady Ruth and the Void Thutin. Unfortunately they both save.

Ixona makes her death save.

Karthis casts Last Rays of the Dying Sun, doing (a maximum of) 48HP of fire then cold damage to all enemies in the room. The remaining non-Void fey duellist dies and, sure enough, another Void creature arises in his place.

When that happens, the Void creatures all around us cackle in glee. We see in each of the pillars around the shape of a playing card appears.

Karthis casts Power Surge on Lady Ruth; this is enough to turn her, the final of the human/fey forms, into a Void creature, wielding a staff.

Galen casts Banishment on five of the creatures (leaving the sixth who was injured by Barrisso). Of those, four are banished.

Gralok attacks Void Thutin; his first attack misses, but the second hits, doing 17HP of slashing damage.

Barrisso makes his death save (using Inspiration).

Void Thutin attacks Norgreath with a tendril, doing 34HP of damage. Norgreath is now down, and the two Void creatures that he banished reappear. Void Thutin's next two attacks hit Gralok, doing 33HP of damage.

The other Void creature phases through Norgreath's simulacrum and then through Karthis, wounding them. It then attacks Galen with a Psychic Orb, doing 27HP of psychic damage.

Norgreath's simulacrum performs a Banishment on the two staff-wielding Void creatures: one is banished.

The real Norgreath fails his death save....
