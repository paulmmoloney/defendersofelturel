# 16 Sep 2026

**[Previously](2026-09-09.md):** We cleared the (no ex-baron) Thutin's castle of his forces, rescued Edmund, the son of the rightful baron. and headed to The Court of the Lunar Knight to vanquish Thutin. There we made our way to its upper level and commenced battle with him and his allies, including Lady Morgant. Karthis led with a Fire Ball that singed a group to our north-east. Lunghiss cast his Shadow Blade and took down the statue-like enemy with one blow. Lady Morgant tried to fell him in retaliation with Moonbeam, but Lunghiss used Spell Thief to cancel its effect. Then an enemy mage used Maddening Darkness to obscure everyone's vision and inflicting psychic damage on the party. Lunghiss badly wounded Lady Morgant, while Galen blinded Thutin and three others using Sunbeam. Thutin then ordered his minions to kill Galen, who survives a knife attack. Gralok killed the enemy archmage, who at that point resurrected as a gigantic tentacle worm-like creature from the Void. Lady Ruth called out. "Heralds! The next sign of the comet has come as planned! Hold your course!", then felled Karthis with her rapier. A shadow fey duellist then struck Barrisso down, while several attacks left Norgreath almost out of health. Norgreath then grabbed the lifeless Karthis and used Thunder Step to escape. The damage done by this kills both Lady Morgant and a shadow fey duellist, who in turn now both resurrect as Void creatures. Lunghiss sneak-attacked Thutin, while Galen used Mass Heal to heal the whole party. Barrisso attempted to use Banishment on the gigantic worm but failed. One of the Void creatures used Collapse Distance to teleport Norgreath right beside the worm, inflicting damage on Karthis who Norgreath had been aiding. Norgreath then cast Banishment on two of the Void creatures and succeeded. The battle continues...
