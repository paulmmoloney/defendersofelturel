# 11 Nov 2026

**[Previously](2026-11-04.md):** After confronting the Mantis and speaking to Lindel, we have travelled to where she told us we might find the Heralds of the Comet: the Leystone of the Indigo Star, on a plane called Lagriss. There we found notes on someone called Askalan and an artefact he spent years studying, the Falling Star, which he spent years studying. Askalan found this place he calls the Leystone of Vale Turog, and used this fallen star in these chambers to bring the place to life. An energy mote from one of the strange devices in this place managed to temporarily possess Lunghiss and used him to cast lightning at the party. We meet a small dragon-like creature, covered in stars, that warns us about its mother. Karthis and Lunghiss attacked and killed the creature and in retaliation, its enraged sibling attacked us. Again, we despatched that. Suddenly, yet another creature tore towards us; this time, a huge tentacled beast, and its partner, both of whose attacks could reduce our intelligence: we defeat both. In a room further on, we found a bronze sphere atop a stone pillar, with a glass canister lying nearby, inside of of which is a perfectly black sphere...

---

We stare at the canister with the sphere. It's about 2' long and 1' wide, cylindrical. Barrisso is wondering whether to take the canister and place it in one of Norgreath's Dimension Doors. Barrisso asks Lunghiss to lift it with a Mage Hand. That works. Then Barrisso looks to see if he can discern any spatial distortion around it, and does not see any effect, nor does Lunghiss.

We move down the corridor towards the double doors, taking the canister with us in a mage hand. As it's a cantrip, he can put down and recast as needed. 

We ask Ixona to have a look and comment how similar it is to her own sphere (that she's lost now). Karthis does an Arcana check, and is sure that it is a Sphere of Annihilation, as we suspected.

The cleric casts a series of heals, first a Prayer of Healing, then two mass heals. And some party members consume potions of healing. Everyone is close to max HP. We gather by the door. Norgreath casts Shadow of Moil, and dispels the web. Barrisso steps forward to open the door.

There's a powerful wind roaring around the vast chamber. The walls of the chamber angle inward as they soar to the full height of this central obelisk, it seems to be 100' high. However, the obelisk is floating and spinning so quickly it's sharp edges are blurred. Near the top is a shadowy form that's circling. Below the obelisk sits a circular disc like a table on a dais, with etched glyphs at the edge. There are two blue tubes connecting the base of the dais to the walls. There's a door in the north-west. The dais is 2' off the ground, the table another 3’, so the bottom of the spinning obelisk is about 5' off the ground level. We suspect the creature is not as solid as other things here. 

Karthis tries to read the glyphs, which triggers a DEX saving throw, and he saves. A bolt of electricity shoots out, taking half damage of 11. Karthis feels it's similar to glyphs of warding. As he's doing that, we ask Lunghiss if he can read the glyphs using Comprehend Languages, but he stops when he sees what has happened to Karthis. Barrisso quaffs a potion of  <to-be-confirmed>.

Barrisso throws an arrow towards the spinning obelisk. The arrow hits the spinning obelisk and is broken. He then flies up a short distance, around 6' up, and stretches his arm across the dais boundary without touching the obelisk. He also has to save DEX, fails, and suffers a bolt of cold for 22 cold damage. 

We consider attacking the pipes. Gralok rages, invokes Call the Hunt, and then using Raggadragga's Maul. He hits the dais first, hitting the ground in front, creating a 10' x 10' x 10' foot trench. The trench runs up to the end of the dais but doesn't affect the dais. This triggers a big recoil requiring a  DEX 25 save. He moves to the north. Karthis casts haste on Barrisso. Galen casts Holy Aura on the party. Barrisso takes a rope, gives one end to Gralok, and flies around the obelisk in a spiral to see what happens. The dais is around 25' in diameter, and he manages to get the rope to catch on the obelisk, and the spinning shreds the rope within seconds. Ixona and Lunghiss skip.

Norgreath casts disintegrate on the tube to the north, and destroys a 10' section of the tube. This triggers a lightning strike of arcane energy in the chamber for all of the party. It extends upwards, but not as high as the creature. Lunghiss evades. Ixona bears the full brunt, 28 full damage. The rest of the party save taking half damage. Gralok heads over towards the other pipe, and wacks down the maul, creating a break in the second pipe. THis triggers another lightning effect and we mostly save. The full damage is 31, and half is 15. Karthis tries a Fire Bolt at the shadowy creature. He hits a critical doing 46 fire damage. It appears to take damage. Galen casts Power Word Fortify, grating 20 temp HP to everyone other than Lunghiss who seems to be able to evade the damage. Barrisso flies up, casting Protection from Energy Lightning. Ixona hits with a crit Fire Bolt doing 54 damage. The shadowy massive creature, a dragon, responds. Everyone must make a DC 22 WIS save. Saves with advantage. Lunghiss uses Inspiration to roll again.

Seems to have a pair of horns that join a sphere of annihilation. The body is formed of the night sky, while it's eyes are like stars. It's heart is like a close up star in its chest.  Its presence is overwhelming. It tries a claw, claw, bite on Barrisso. Slashing and cold, slashing and cold, piercing and cold. Total damage was 87 damage, but with resistance to slashing he is down to 28 HP.

Lunghiss is frightened but tries to fire a shortbow even though it's over 80' away. He missed. Before Norgreath can act, the dragon whips its tail against Barrisso, 15 bludgeoning leaving him on 13. Norgreath tries his traditional Eldritch Blast. Two of the four blasts hit, and he pushes the target 10' away (further up) each time. The dragon tries to hit with the tail but just misses. Gralok hops on the dais to attack the obelisk. He is hit with lightning, doing 16 HP. He hits with his maul. He hits, and feels he impacted the spin. He's hurled backwards and hits the far wall. He takes 18 bludgeoning damage (halved as he is resistant when raging). He's dazed. He steps back via the same glyph. This time he takes 26 HP of fire damage from the glyph. He then hits the obelisk again. He thinks it affects the speed of the rotation, but he's hurled away again, taking 12 bludgeoning. Karthis casts a fireball, centring it above the dragon to avoid Barrisso.It does seem to do damage. Galen casts heal on Barrisso, but delays until he's in range. The dragon succeeds in hitting Barrisso and knocks him out, Barrisso falls. When he hits 60' from Galen he gets 70 HP.
