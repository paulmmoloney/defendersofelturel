# 20 May 2026

**[Previously](2026-05-14.md):** We recovered a fragment of the Tyrant of War key in The Infinite Labyrinth, after defeating a black dragon and looting two nearby treasure chests. We then used Norgreath's coin to travel to the next destination where we hope to find a fragment: somewhere (?) called Ramiah the Star Blade. We found ourselves in a region of metallic surfaces, inhabited by strange entities composed of mismatched segments of crudely forged metal, calling themselves the Embodied. They are souls that have travelled to this place and gained bodies of a type. One told us to talk to someone called The Sovereign in a place called the City of Souls for more information. On meeting the Sovereign, they offered us a bargain: information if we rid this place of someone called the Empty Prince. This being is gathering together an army of Shadows: Shadows are those who have failed the trial of Embodiment, and who now haunt this region. Something called the Empty Star will destroy any souls who pass through it, and the Empty Prince believes all should give up their Embodiment and go to the Empty Star. We travelled to a massive iron fortress at the edge of the world (for it is a flat disk) and there met the Empty Prince. However, our parlay went badly very quickly, and he and his clockwork minions are attacking us...

---

Norgreath first flies through the air, then casts Synaptic Static centred on the Empty Prince. Anyone inside the sphere takes 24HP of psychic damage plus the other effects of the spell.

Lunghiss casts Shadow Blade, then sneak attacks the Empty Prince with his new weapon using Savage Attacker and Booming Blade, doing 61HP of damage (plus 16HP if they move).

The Empty Prince casts Lightning Bolt towards the party, doing a max of 16HP.

Gralok rages and uses Call the Hunt, then attacks the Empty Prince with his Blazing Cold Greataxe, doing 17HP of damage.

The Empty Prince does a bash attack against Gralok but misses. His second attack on Barrisso connects, doing 14HP bludgeoning plus 29HP of necrotic damage.

Ixona of the White Eye flips up her eyepatch. She removes a white orb from the socket underneath, and throws it into the air. As it flies towards one of the clockwork minions, it grows into a two-foot diameter sphere. When it hits, it does 53HP of damage.

Galen casts Holy Aura on the party.

The minion just hit by Ixona's orb attacks Barrisso and Gralok with a jet of alchemical fire, doing 13HP of fire damage.

Another clockwork myrmidon attacks Karthis, doing 14HP of bludgeoning damage.

The next beside Gralok attacks him but misses.

The fourth myrmidon moves, taking an attack of opportunity from Gralok, taking 15HP of slashing damage, killing him.
