# 20 May 2026

**[Previously](2026-05-14.md):** We recovered a fragment of the Tyrant of War key in The Infinite Labyrinth, after defeating a black dragon and looting two nearby treasure chests. We then used Norgreath's coin to travel to the next destination where we hope to find a fragment: somewhere (?) called Ramiah the Star Blade. We found ourselves in a region of metallic surfaces, inhabited by strange entities composed of mismatched segments of crudely forged metal, calling themselves the Embodied. They are souls that have travelled to this place and gained bodies of a type. One told us to talk to someone called The Sovereign in a place called the City of Souls for more information. On meeting the Sovereign, they offered us a bargain: information if we rid this place of someone called the Empty Prince. This being is gathering together an army of Shadows: Shadows are those who have failed the trial of Embodiment, and who now haunt this region. Something called the Empty Star will destroy any souls who pass through it, and the Empty Prince believes all should give up their Embodiment and go to the Empty Star. We travelled to a massive iron fortress at the edge of the world (for it is a flat disk) and there met the Empty Prince. However, our parlay went badly very quickly, and he and his clockwork minions are attacking us...

Norgreath first flies through the air, then casts Synaptic Static centred on the Empty Prince. Anyone inside the sphere takes 24HP of psychic damage plus the other effects of the spell.

Lunghiss casts Shadow Blade, then sneak attacks the Empty Prince with his new weapon using Savage Attacker and Booming Blade, doing 61HP of damage (plus 16HP if they move).

The Empty Prince casts Lightning Bolt towards the party, doing a maximum of 16HP.

Gralok rages and uses Call the Hunt, then attacks the Empty Prince with his Blazing Cold Greataxe, doing 17HP of damage.

The Empty Prince does a bash attack against Gralok but misses. His second attack on Barrisso connects, doing 14HP bludgeoning plus 29HP of necrotic damage.

Ixona of the White Eye flips up her eyepatch. She removes a white orb from the socket underneath, and throws it into the air. As it flies towards one of the clockwork minions, it grows into a two-foot diameter sphere. When it hits, it does 53HP of damage.

Galen casts Holy Aura on the party.

The minion just hit by Ixona's orb attacks Barrisso and Gralok with a jet of alchemical fire, doing 13HP of fire damage.

Another clockwork myrmidon attacks Karthis, doing 14HP of bludgeoning damage.

The next beside Gralok attacks him but misses.

The fourth myrmidon moves, taking an attack of opportunity from Gralok, taking 15HP of slashing damage, killing him.

---

After we kill the last of the clockwork creatures, Galen casts a Prayer of Healing. Barrisso searches the body of the Empty Prince. But, as his name suggests, there’s nothing of substance. However, as Barrisso is looking at the remains of the construct, he checks for perception. What he sees is a tear-shaped ectoplasmic form, rising up from the remains and travelling in the direction of the Empty Star.

We were sent by the Sovereign to remove the Empty Prince: it seems we have achieved that.

We are healed, and we all get 27HP back as healing. Barrisso and Gralok need some more healing. Barrisso heals himself. Galen sorts Gralok with +30 HP. Now we explore the other doors here. We start with the door to the south. There is a privy and an anteroom to the south-west – almost like a gatehouse for someone to live in. It’s abandoned, and contains an old bed, a table, some shelves, some pots and pans. It is dusty and uninteresting.

We move on to the next door to the south-east and look around the rooms beyond. There are a series of chambers that seem to be largely bare of normal furniture. We look behind the big wall hangings/curtains: nothing much to see here. We do notice the dust is a bit metallic. We see pieces of metal and little boxes of metal, a piston or two, some cogs and some nails. Looks like some constructs were hanging out here.

At the back is a big room with a statue-like plinth now empty: probably where one of the creatures came out to attack us. Some smaller, inanimate smaller statues remain. In the top right corner are stairs upwards. We don’t go up yet. We explore the rest of chambers and find nothing of interest: just more spare parts. To the south is another set of stairs upwards.

Before going up, we all take a short rest, particularly useful for Norgreath. It’s a bare space but a great view of the Empty Star. We all need to make a wisdom saving throw. We all succeed, but we feel an urge to go towards the Empty Star. Taq is under Norgreath’s control, so even though he fails, Norgreath instructs him not to go.

We return to the Sovereign. We head up the stairs hanging from the many-sided metal structure to talk to her. "As requested, we have dispatched the Empty Prince," says Barrisso. The Sovereign sighs. "The dream of Shaziad can safely continue, though I do miss her."

"In accordance with your request, you promised the shard, the segment of the key. May we have it?" asks Barrisso.

"I did promise this, and I will grant it. Is there anything else you would like to achieve whilst you are here?"

"Of what do you speak, Sovereign?" asks Barrisso.

"I was the very first embodied created by Shaziad. And that is why this place is called Ramiah, or to give its full name, Ramiah’s Blade, or the Star Blade of Ramiah" she says. "You have earned the right to an abode in my reality should you wish it."

"That is very generous of you. Is there any consequence to abiding here that we should be aware of?"

"I was given the chance to have a real existence in this place which could have been a prison once I was touched by the blade. All of my followers are in a similar situation. We enjoy our existence here, and we reject the nothingness of the Empty Star."

"We accept your offer, but not just yet."

"Could we have a teleport circle to return again?" asks Norgreath.

"I do not have the ability to do this", she says.

She reaches down to a drawer under her throne and withdraws a piece of the Tyrant’s Key and holds it out to Barrisso. Barrisso asks who in the party does not yet have a segment. Gralok says he doesn’t. He takes it. We bid the Sovereign goodbye.

We decide to return to Elturel to recharge. Using Norgreath’s Plane Shift, we all return to his quarters in Elturel, using the Teleportation Circle as an anchor. We plan to have some wine and cheese, and some other refreshments before having a long rest.

**Everyone goes up to Level 16.**
