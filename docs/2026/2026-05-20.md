# 20 May 2026

**[Previously](2026-05-14.md):** We recovered an fragment of the Tyrant of War key in The Infinite Labyrinth, after defeating a black dragon and looting two nearby treasure chests. We then used Norgreath's coin to travel to the next destination where we hope to find a fragment: somewhere (?) called Ramiah the Star Blade. We found ourselves in a region of metallic surfaces, inhabited by strange entities composed of mismatched segments of crudely forged metal, calling themselves the Embodied. They are souls that have travelled to this place and gained bodies of a type. One told us to talk to someone called The Sovereign in a place called the City of Souls for more information. On meeting the Sovereign, they offered us a bargain: information if we rid this place of someone called the Empty Prince. This being is gathering together an army of Shadows: Shadows are those who have failed the trial of Embodiment, and who now haunt this region. Something called the Empty Star will destroy any souls who pass through it, and the Empty Prince believes all should give up their Embodiment and go to the Empty Star. We travelled to a massive iron fortress at the edge of the world (for it is a flat disk) and there met the Empty Prince. However, our parlay went badly very quickly, and he and his clockwork minions are attacking us...
