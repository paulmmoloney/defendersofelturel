# 11 Mar 2026

**[Previously](2026-03-04.md):** Maon's sentient book told us that the Dragon Doom is a shard of the petrified Heart of the Giant, Xeluan, whose corpse is located in a cavern in the hills. We went to there and fought Aminta, leader of the guards of the cavern, and the doppelgangers she had hired to impersonate and guard her. Once she knew she was beaten, she surrendered, leaving the caverns to us. We soon forced her elven mage to surrender after we defeated his golem. He explained that he had been gathering shards of the giant to capture souls. And some of the souls he captured were the band of heroes depicted in tapestries and murals in the tomb, who had aided the giant Xeluan. When the shards containing those souls are placed against the frozen giant, the souls are released. In the burial chamber of the caverns, we find the ghost of Rilago. By Barrisso allowing himself to be possessed by Rilago, he is able to merge the Dragon Doom with the giant. It is now no more.

---

He decide to leave and head back to the keep. As soon as we step out the main iron doors through which we originally entered, we encounter four revenants. we recognise three of them as Tycho's champions, Isolde, Torven, and Jaxen, now undead. The fourth one is unknown to us. They wield giant swords, but their armor looks dishevelled.

Isolde: "Tycho - we've found them."

The one that we don't recognise, and is certainly not Tycho, says: "Let us finish this!"

Karthis casts Fireball at them, doing a max of 40HP of damage. He adds extra damage to the pseudo-Tycho using a Power Surge.

Gralok uses Call the Hunt, then attacks with his Blazing Cold Greataxe, doing 18HP of damage to pseudo-Tycho. Pseudo-Tycho seems to look like an old farmer's wife. Has Tycho someone inhabited a new body?

Norgreath casts his Eldritch Blast (with Agonizing Blast), with the first with Genie's Wrath (Cold damage) at pseudo-Tycho, doing 43HP of damage.

Galen uses Channel Divinity to perform Order's Demand to get them to drop their weapons. Nothing happens.

Isolde attacks Galen, doing 30HP of damage.

Torven attacks Gralok, wounding him.

Jaxen attacks Barrisso, doing 34HP of damage.

Pseudo-Tycho also attacks Gralok.

Barrisso attacks pseudo-Tycho, doing 43HP of damage. His second scimitar attack is parried. His offhand attack does 27HP of damage.

Lunghiss casts Mage Hand so that next turn he can use Versatile Trickster and give himself advantage. He then does a sneak attack on pseudo-Tycho, with Lathander's Radiant Blade, using Booming Blade and Savage Attacker. He does 55HP of damage - plus a tiny 2HP thunder damage if he subsequently moves.

---

Karthis casts Fire Bolt at pseudo-Tycho, doing 33HP of damage. The creature falls.

Gralok activates his Blazing Cold Axe and attacks Isolde, doing 43HP of damage.

Norgreath casts Synaptic Static at all the enemies, unfortunately also getting some of the party. Those who fail their save take full damage and must subtract from their saves.

Galen casts Sunbeam at two of the enemies: one takes 31HP of damage, and dies. The other takes half damage.

Isolde casts Hunter's Mark on Norgreath, then attacks with her sword - but misses.

Torven casts Hunter's Mark on Gralok, then attacks, doing 42HP of damage.

Barrisso attacks Torven, doing 22HP of damage. He attacks again, doing 15HP of damage, killing him. He moves to Isolde and attacks with his offhand, doing 15HP of damage.

Lunghiss moves in and finishes Isolde off.

---

Norgreath casts Demiplane and moves the four revenant bodies into a demiplane. We check the bodies for valuables, but find nothing.

We go up into the level that had the murals, and we take a long rest.

---

The next day, we head back to the keep. A few hours later, we reach it, and meet with Maon and Real. We tell them about what has happened to the Dragon Doom.

"We merged the Dragon Doom with the frozen form of Xeluan. It should be able to have no more effect."

"Oh this is great news!" she says.

"Maybe we can consult the book, and see its reaction, and see what we can do about restoring dragonkind."

We cross over to the book, on its usual stand.

"The Dragon Doom banished dragons, is that correct?"

"Yes."

"What would it take to get those dragons back?"

"I don't know."

It seems the book is of no help. We ask Maon for her advice.

"Now the shard is merged, it no longer prevents the dragons returning."

"How do we get them back?"

"It seems we have to find them and ask them to return. Or somehow send them back from another plane."

"If this is their native plane, they should be free to return."

Real says: "I have no idea how to call them from another plane."

Galen casts Commune to ask his deity some questions.

He first asks, "Are the dragons banished to a single plane?"

"NO."

"Is Real's father still alive?"

"YES."

"Do we have any means to restore the dragons to this plane?"

"UNCLEAR."

Galen uses Divine Intervention. He gets no response.

Norgreath tries to commune with his patron using his font. Its waters swirl violently and it seems to Norgreath that the patterns on its surface are giant waves somewhere else. He feels a physic connection.

"Master I call you in our hour of need."

"AND?"

"And I have some gifts."

"Hmmph. What are they."

"Two magical items from a plane of fire."

"Fiery things?"

"Hmmph."

"I also have three dragon teeth."

"Do you also have a goat's arse?"

"I'm sorry my gifts are not tempting, my master."

"Where is that angel's sword I asked for?"

"Unfortunately, that was consumed."

*sigh* "In that case, give me your fiery gifts."

Norgreath does so in a demiplane, and feels his patron's watery presence.

"On this strange plane, magic was dying out. Magical creatures such as dragons have been banished. We now wish to return them to this plane. With your great powers, can you help us, oh master?"

"I do not know directly. However, are you saying that all those banished are dragons?"

"I believe so."

"There is a creature that you might find, but I do not know exactly where. This creature is the Oracle of Dragonkind: an ancient sapphire dragon. If any creature in the Multiverse knows where dragonkind have been dwelling, it would be this oracle."

Norgreath *(Arcana check)* is aware of a type of dragon called gem dragons, which are rarer than chromatics or metallics. Sapphire dragons tend to be neutral in good/evil alignment. The one he refers to is Marithidran, who is rumoured to have traded its eyes for enhanced prophecy.

We decide to go to sleep.

---

The next morning, Galen attempts Divine Intervention again. He feels a holy presence surrounding him.

"Can you help me restore the dragons to this realm?"

"I can, if that is your wish."

"It is."

We see a huge glowing left hand appear in front of us. We hear a voice, "Ignes! Fatoon! Come from Legathon!".

A doorway opens, allowing the sounds of howling winds through it. Through the doorway appears a large blue dragon, its head waving violently from side to side. It fixes its eyes upon us. It doesn't look pleased.

Meanwhile, another portal opens. Through it can be seen a sun-dappled glade. We hear another voice. "Silvara! Vatbringer! Come". A black dragon wyrmling appears through the portal.

A third circular door opens, from Mechanus. "Vispira! Silverwing! Come!". We see a giant brass cage being lifted off a young silver dragon.

A fourth portal opens into Celestia. We can see directly to a beautiful peaceful mountain slope. Around an ancient red dragon there, there are a hoard of angels. "Gorthax the Glutton! Come!".

Finally, the last portal opens into a gloom: one of the layers of Hades. Sitting with its scales losing their colour, surrounded by demonic larvae, we see an adult bronze dragon. The hand points towards it.

"Subonix, the Patient - your time of torture is over. Come to your homeland."

He rouses himself and comes through the portal.

Suddenly in front of us are now five dragons, two of which looks scary. Real calls out in delight to see Subonix, presumably his father.

We decide to leave. Norgreath says in Draconic: "Be good to each other. This plane needs dragonkind to restore magic."

Norgreath casts Plane Shift. [We violently shift to our original plane...](2026-03-18.md)
