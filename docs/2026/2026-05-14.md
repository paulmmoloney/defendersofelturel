# 14 May 2026

**[Previously](2026-05-06.md):** The latest fragment of the Tyrant of War key has led us to a place known as The Infinite Labyrinth. It was once a city called Navatar in the land of Scryon, once ruled by Kaos, but now contains a labyrinth around his tomb which expands magically while the rest of the city shrinks. Here, we fought boneshard wraiths, a clockwork abomination, clockwork beetles, and mechanical ballistae. We were teleported to an obsidian bridge crossing a bottomless void, where we defeated a great flying lizard made of shadow. Through a portal, we found ourselves in what felt like the comforting confines of a tavern, where we fought some monstrous ochre jellies, explored rooms and discovered a full-size black dragon guarding treasure...

---

We gather together and discuss what to do with the dragon. Norgreath attempts to cast Banishment on it. However, the spell fails and instead the creature starts to rouse itself from its slumber.

Barrisso flies at top speed towards it, and uses Channel Divinity: Vow of Enmity on it. He also attacks it, doing 32HP damage. Using Luck, he succeeds in hitting the dragon again, doing 29HP of damage.

The dragon leaps towards the middle of the room and performs a Rend attack on Barrisso, doing 21HP of damage. The party is nauseated by the acidic smell of the dragon.

Ixona is stunned: "What the hell is that?"

Karthis casts Haste on Gralok.

Gralok rages and uses his Call the Hunt ability. He runs towards the dragon and swings his Blazing Cold Greataxe at it, doing 16HP of damage.

The dragon summons a cloud of insects at Gralok, taking 22HP of poison damage.

Norgreath casts Eldritch Blast with Agonizing Blast and Genie's Wrath, doing 24HP of damage. His follow-up attacks do another 11HP of damage.

Lunghiss (using Inspiration) performs a sneak attack with Lathander's Radiant Blade (using Savage Attacker), doing 40HP of damage (plus 9HP if it moves).

The dragon does indeed move towards the bulk of the party, attacking Gralok, doing 14HP of damage.

Galen casts Sacred Flame at it, but it misses.

The dragon screams at us in a guttural voice, flecks of spit flying from its fangs. "WHO DARES TO DISTURB THE REST OF VORASHAN!?"

The dragon makes an acid breath attack on the party: Galen takes 54HP damage, Norgreath takes 27HP. Lunghiss does a backflip, giving the dragon the finger.

---

Barrisso flies back to the dragon and attacks, doing 25HP of damage. With his second attack, he does 33HP. When the second attack connects, there is a gout of acid blood from the wound. Both Barrisso and Gralok are caught in this spray, and both take 10HP of damage.

But - the dragon is dead!

---

We explore the chests that the dragon had been resting near. We find:

- Three gold tankards, worth 50GP each
- 3,573 gold pieces
- 10,429 silver pieces
- 20,000+ copper pieces
- ...and another key segment
- A magical cloak

Karthis casts Identify, and discovers that the fine cloak of black material with silvery threads is a Cloak of Arachnida, enabling the wearer to spider-climb and cast Web.

Galen casts Prayer of Healing, giving everyone 42HP of healing back.

Norgreath casts Demiplane to find a place to store the carcass of the black dragon, plus the treasure we found. (Since the entrance to the demiplane is too small, we unfortunately have to cut up the body.)

Before we travel onward to the next destination where we expect to find a key fragment, we decide to take a long rest. Barrisso uses True Sight and Lunghiss checks the room: there seem to be no other secret entrances. We spike the door and take our rest.

---

After we awaken, Norgreath uses his coin so we can travel to the next location where we hope to find a segment of the key for the Tyrant of War vehicle: one of the last two phrases we have is "Ramiah the Star Blade". Norgreath concentrates, and a misty path appears in front of us, leading up and out of the chamber. We follow this silvery trail and are transported. As we leave, we realise we didn't help Misara with her task...

We find ourselves floating over a strange landscape: buildings below us are actually themselves floating in the sky, held up by what look like balloons. As we travel on, we see a greasy river, fouled with corpses and bones, winding through a grey dismal landscape. We continue on. Out of this area of permanent shadow, we find ourselves in a dazzling area of great cliffs of translucent layers, soaring infinitely high above us.

Finally, we see a side path to which Norgreath's token pulls him. We arrive in an area of hard metallic surfaces, broken up by canyon-sized fissures. At our back is a pile of house-sized metallic blocks fused together to form a tower. The sky is filled with stars and nebulae of every colour - except for a blotch on the horizon. Nearby, we see a dozen entities made of mismatched segments of crudely forged metal. Half of the group carry metal poles with loops at one end. An ectoplasmic film stretches across the loops.

When we appear, some of these beings freeze in place, as if startled. Others seem to whisper to each other.

As we follow the lead of the key segments, one of those beings, with a strange resemblance to a dog (but with too many legs) hesitantly approaches us and speaks.

"Greetings, embodied ones. Do you want to undergo formal embodiment?"

"Er, no thanks."

"What is your purpose here?"

"Tell us about 'formal embodiment'".

"Most of us don't arrive in physical form. We have been granted these bodies through, well, embodiment."

"Is this an afterlife?"

"I don't think so. We are, I guess, souls, but this is not an afterlife. Our souls travelled here without form."

"Do you remember your prior life?"

"Somewhat."

"Are you all from the same place?"

"We're not too sure."

"Have you heard of Ramiah the Star Blade?"

They gasp.

"Well, most of us were taken by the Blade. Now we live with it."

"Where is this Blade?"

It spreads its limb to encompass the whole area.

"We try to understand it as this whole place. We were drawn here: most of us remember being slain by the Blade and trapped here. That is until another being arrived in their own flesh. Shaziad? She helped us find our forms."

"Is Ramiah a person?"

It seems to have been a blade, but the blade was wielded by different beings. Shaziad is gone long ago.

"Is there a way of leaving here?"

This question upsets the entity.

Barrisso shows the fragment of the key and says we are looking for something similar.

"Maybe you should speak to the Sovereign in the City of Souls?"

He points in the direction in which our key fragments are also pulling us.

"What are the hoops you carry for?"

"The disembodied."

We bid farewell and head east where a path across the metal plane, shiny from foot traffic, leads to the end of a fissure. Quarry marks are etched into the sides of the canyon. We continue down into the fissure, which eventually opens into a space with giant metal spars above us. From those spars hang  lighted paths and buildings glowing with light. We see many of the Embodied around, some congregating on a bowl-like platform.

We find that we are a source of curiosity as we pass. We come to a many-sided metal structure hanging by a wire from bridges high above. A being is sitting at the top of these stairs: they look rusted in places, and unlike the other creatures who seem to wear a mask, they have the sculpted face of a woman. On each side of the staircase are other Embodied, like a court surrounding a leader. They do not seem to be too antagonistic. This being we find out is called the Sovereign.

"I welcome you to the City of Souls - what is your purpose here?"

We explain about the key.

"Ah! I see why you are here, and what you seek. Perhaps I can provide that for you. If... you can do something for me?"

The Shadows are those who have failed the trial of Embodiment. Once they have failed, they haunt the plains. But they have been gathered under the command of the Empty Prince, the prophet of the Empty Star and ultimate destruction. Can you rid us of this Empty Prince?"

We are slightly baffled, and wonder if we are even able?

"There", she says, pointing across the landscape. "That is the Empty Star that will destroy any souls who pass through it. The Empty Prince believes we should give up our Embodiment and go to the Empty Star. We would cling to our bodies. Remove this negative evil creature that preys upon our folk. "

"Your souls are safe and will not be drawn to the Empty Star."

---

We head in the direction of the Empty Prince, guided by the nervous dog-faced creature we met initially, called Gambol. As we travel, we realise that this world is flat, and we are walking towards the edge. We also notice a massive iron fortress standing on the cliff edge. Its many open windows and doors are filled with shadow. Behind it, the starscape is marred by a hole in the sky, darker than any black.

We can see that this fortress has a main courtyard entrance. In there we find a large Embodied. This seems to be the Empty Prince. They have a bearded male face. Around him are several others, who look like the clockwork creatures we found in the Infinite Labyrinth, but more crude in form. Each of these has the image of the Empty Star etched into their masks.

He greets us: "Have you come on your journey to the Empty Star?"

"Yes."

"Do you need us to show you the way?"

"Actually we need help."

We show him a key segment and explain our quest.

"But there is nothing left but the Empty Star?. There is no hope."

"I am alive and we come from a place where others are alive."

"You are deceiving yourself. There is no hope. There is no saving. You must come to peace."

"What do you call this existence?"

"We live on the edge of the blade, contemplating the nothingness, guiding the souls to the star."

"I am not of your opinion. We need to find the key. Will you help or hinder us?"

The Empty Prince does not like these words: he and his clockwork minions attack.

Barrisso attacks, doing 33HP of damage with Divine Smite. As soon as he attacks, the Empty Prince throws up an Arcane Ward which absorbs some of the damage. His second attack does 37HP damage. His third attack does 30HP of damage.

Taq, Norgreath's familiar, moves south.

Karthis moves and casts Last Rays of the Dying Sun, dealing a maximum of 40HP to all enemies.

In response, the Empty Prince casts Symbol. [The party take 25HP of necrotic damage...](2026-05-20.md)
