# 23 Sep 2026

**[Previously](2026-09-16.md):** We cleared the baronial castle of Thutin and his forces and tracked him down to The Court of the Lunar Knight. In the ensuing battle, when our human enemies fell, they resurrected as bizarre creatures from the Void. Gralok killed Thutin himself, who was replaced by the most gigantic Void creature we had seen so far. First Karthis then Ixona were downed; the latter reduced to an imbecile by Void creature attacks. Norgreath cast Simulacrum; his doppelganger tried and failed to banish Lady Ruth and Thutin. One human enemy remained, Lady Ruth, who Karthis killed using Last Rays of the Dying Sun. At that point, all our enemies were strange tentacled creatures. Galen managed to cast Banishment successfully on four of them, but Barrisso succumbed due to multiple attacks from Void Thutin. Void Thutin then downed Norgreath who as a result lost concentration, allowing the two Void creatures he had banished to reappear. But Norgreath's simulacrum is still around, and managed to banish one of the enemy....

---

Ixona makes her death save.

Lunghiss sneak attacks the nearest Void creature using his Shadow Blade and Booming Blade (with Savage Attacker). He hits, but the target somehow warps to the far side of the room, escaping damage.

Karthis casts Lightning Bolt at three of our opponents, doing a max of 29HP of damage; Karthis adds an extra 8HP of damage to the first target using Power Surge.

Galen casts Toll the Dead on an enemy near Karthis. He almost does 24HP of necrotic damage, but unfortunately the creature saves.

One of the Void creatures moves and performs bite and tendril attacks on Karthis, doing 56HP, who is grappled in the beast's maw. Karthis is almost dead *(3HP)*, but uses Inspiration to avoid his maximum hit point total being reduced which would have downed him.

Gralok is targetted by Void Thutin with a desire of Insatiable Hunger, and suddenly feels he wants to eat the downed body of Barrisso. He bites his fallen comrade doing 7HP of damage.

Thw now chewed Barrisso succeeds his death save.

Void Thutin moves towards Galen and attacks three times; one hits, for 32HP of damage. Galen our cleric is down.

Another Void creature makes a Psychic Orb attack on Karthis, but misses.

With four of the party down and one on the verge, we decide we need to escape.

Norgreath's Simulacrum uses Limited Wish to cast a Heal spell on the real, fallen, Norgreath, giving him 70HP.

Before the real Norgreath can stand up, the whole chamber seems to undulate in form, and the card shapes we saw earlier in the pillars start to glow.

Norgreath holds an action to cast Plane Shift on any of the party who are near each other clasping hands, giving us time to get near each other.

Ixona makes a death save, but fails.

Lunghiss runs over to the fallen Galen and administers a Potion of Greater Healing on him, restoring him to life with 13HP of health. He then moves to stand beside Norgreath and Ixona and clasps their hands.

The grappled Karthis casts Dimension Door and teleports to beside Lunghiss, grabbing his band.

The restored Galen casts Cure Wounds on himself, giving himself another 34HP of health. But as he moves away, Void Thutin gets an attack of opportunity, downing him again. He falls near to the rest of the party but not near enough to grasp a hand and join their escape.

Norgreath's Plane Shift kicks in, and six of the party are whisked away back to Norgreath's teleportation circle in Elturel, leaving Galen, Taq, and Norgreath's simulacrum behind.

A Void creature bites Norgreath's simulacrum, doing 24HP of damage and grappling it. It then follows up with a tendril attack.

The Void Thutin attacks Norgreath's simulacrum, downing it, with no possibility of being healed. It follows up with an attack of Taq, squashing it. The last attack is on the lone Galen...

---

Back in Elturel. Barrisso fortunately succeeds on his next death save, and is now stable. Ixona is also stable, and we administer a Potion of Healing to her. Barrisso also heals himself. We wait for hopefully Norgreath's simulacrum to also return with Galen. But after a while we think that maybe they are not coming back.

We decided to interrogate Ixona about what has happened. She is after all the reason we ended up fighting Thutin and his now Void-warped companions. But is shell-shocked by everything that has happened. She has assumed it was a simple fight with a human usurper who had killed her family.

We take two long rests to fully recover. Then Norgreath uses his magic coin to return us back to Ixona's world K'thar. As we travel along the silvery path it creates, the first plane we encounter is like a world of liquid with a fortress of coral, with fishy humanoids carrying tridents and wearing coral armour. The path continues on and we leave that area into a stranger zone with no solid ground. We are surrounded by glowing filaments that extend in all directions. Where they gather, huge blinking crystals are suspended from their junctions. We continue along until a sidepath off the main path leads us down to the ground outside the baronial keep where we had previously been. We hide in the woods and sent the invisible Taq into to see if everything is normal or has the Void creature ceremony had an impact here. 

Some things have changed. The gatehouses have fallen and the main gate is down. The inner gate has also fallen. There are bodies strewn around. Taq enters the keep and does not find anyone alive. He cannot see the restore baron, not yet anyway. He continues up a level and finds it abandoned. Every so often he finds instead of a door or wall just a hole. 

He goes down to the basement level. Again, there is no one around and the ship is gone. While we are communicating from the woods with Taq, we notice that the sky is turning shades of purple and green. Norgreath recalls Taq, and uses Scrying to try and find the large Void Thutin. Instead, he takes 31HP of psychic damage...
