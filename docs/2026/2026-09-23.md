# 23 Sep 2026

**[Previously](2026-09-016.md):** We cleared the baronial castle of Thutin and his forces, and tracked him down to The Court of the Lunar Knight. In the ensuing battle, when human enemies have fallen, they have resurrected as bizarre creatures from the Void. Gralok killed Thutin himself, so was the replaced by the most gigantic Void creature we had seen so far.  First Karthis, then Ixona were downed; the latter reduced to an imbecile by Void creature attacks. Norgreath cast Simulacrum; his doppelganger tried and failed to banish Lady Ruth and Thutin. One human enemy remained, Lady Ruth, who Karthis killed using Last Rays of the Dying Sun. Now all our enemies were strange tentacled creatures. Galen managed to cast Banishment successfully on four of them, but Barrisso succumbed due to multiple attacks from Void Thutin. Void Thutin then downed Norgreath, and losing concentration, the two Void creatures he had banished reappeared. But Norgreath's simulacrum is still around, and managed to banish one of the enemy (five in total are currently banished...)

---

Ixona makes her death save.

Lunghiss sneak attacks the nearest Void creature using his Shadow Blade and Booming Blade (with Savage Attacker). He hits, but the target somehow warps to the far side of the room, escaping damage.

Karthis casts Lightning Bolt at three of our opponents, doing a max of 29HP of fire damage; Karthis adds an extra 8HP of damage to the first target using Power Surge.

Galen casts Toll the Dead on an enemy near Karthis. He would done 24HP of necrotic damage, but unfortunately the creature saves.

One of the Void creatures moves and performs bite and tendrils attacks on Karthis, doing 56HP, and is grappled by the beast. Karthis is almost dead *(3HP)*. He uses Inspiration to avoid his maximum hit point total being reduced.

Gralok is targetted by Void Thutin with a desire of Insatiable Hunger, and suddenly feels he wants to eat the downed body of Barrisso. He bites, doing 7HP of damage.

Barrisso succeeds his death save.

The Void Thutin moves towards Galen and attacks three times; one hits, for 32HP of damage. Galen is down.

Another Void creature makes a Psychic Orb attack on Karthis, but misses.

We decide we need to escape, with four down and one almost down.

Norgreath's Simulacrum uses Limited Wish to cast a Heal spell on the real, fallen, Norgreath, giving him 70HP.

Before the real Norgreath can stand up, the whole chamber seems to undulate in form, and the card shapes we saw earlier in the pillars start to glow.

Norgreath holds an action to cast Plane Shift on any of the party who are near and clasping each other.

Ixona makes a death save, but fails.

Lunghiss moves to the fallen Galen and administers a Potion of Greater Healing on him, restoring him to life with 13HP of health. He then moves to stand beside Norgreath and Ixona and clasp their hands.

The grappled Karthis casts Dimension Door and lands beside Lunghiss, grabbing his band.

The restored Galen casts Cure Wounds on himself, giving himself another 34HP of health. But as he moves away, Void Thutin gets an attack of opportunity, downing him again. He falls near to the rest of the party but not near enough to hold a hand.

Norgreath's Plane Shift kicks in, and six of the party are whisked away back to Norgreath's teleportation circle in Elturel, leaving Galen, Taq, and Norgreath's simulacrum behind.

A Void creature bites Norgreath's simulacrum, doing 24HP of damage and grappling it. It then follows up with a tendril attack.

The Void Thutin attacks Norgreath's simulacrum, downing it, with no possibility of being healed. It follows up with an attack of Taq, squashing it. The last attack is on the lone Galen...

---

Back in Elturel. Barrisso fortunately succeeds on his next death save, and is now stable. Ixona is also stable, and we administer a Potion of Healing to her. We wait for hopefully Norgreath's simulacrum to also return with Galen. But after a while we think that maybe they are not coming back.
