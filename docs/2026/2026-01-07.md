# 7 Jan 2026

**[Previously](../2025/2025-12-18.md):** After a disastrous attempt to attack Tycho in his fort, the party had to retreat and lost Barrisso and Gralok, who were both executed. Galen has resurrected both from their recovered heads. Lunghiss scouts the city: the residents are calm and there seems no danger of us being discovered in our lair. Our helper, Crom Picket, gives us food, drink, and equipment for Gralok and Barrisso, and we spend a number of days recovering from exhaustion. We plan a second attack to retrieve the first element of the Tyrant of War’s key from Tycho. Taq returns to the fort to scout it, and finds no one inside. From an overheard conversation, he finds out they have moved to assault the valley where the magic users are holding out.


---

As we debate our plan – whether to try and retrieve the fallen party members’ equipment, or whether to head straight to the magic creatures, who we think have engaged in battle.

Crom comes to our hideout. He tells us of knowledge he has that the artifact is active for half a day, and is re-enabled at dawn the following day. The artefact is made from parts of primordial, ancient enemies of dragons. To destroy it it needs to be recombined with the petrified primordial holding up a vault.

Gralok still has one and two levels of exhaustion. Barrisso has one level.

Taq tells us there are 6 guards approaching. So we made a quick escape plan. Everyone, except Barrisso, goes into Norgreath’s ring, and Taq takes it invisibility. Barrisso used a dimension door, with Crom, to get out of the house. 

As we depart the guards break down the door. But we don’t know what they say or do, as we’re gone.

---

Barrisso and Crom go to the old temple. Then Crom makes his own way onwards, and Barrisso goes upwards and flies to our agreed rendezvous point at the edge of the city, where Taq becomes visible so they meet up and fly onwards together.  Barrisso flies as high as he can so he can minimise the chance of being seen.

They land at the end of the day, and everyone can get out of the ring, and have a long rest. At this point it makes sense to give the Hat of Disguise to Barrisso, so he can use it to disguise himself as if he were an eagle (or as close as can be done). Eamon casts Greater Restoration to remove one level of exhaustion.  Then we have a long rest, and the final exhaustion level for Barrisso and Gralok is relieved. We have our two elves meditating keeping an eye. Taq can also keep watch.

The following day we prepare to do the same. Barrisso now can create an approximation of a disguise as an eagle. The rest of the party join Norgreath in his sanctuary vessel. Taq carries the ring, and is invisible, and tracks Barrisso. Again they fly high to minimise chances of detection. We track the river up the valley.

The next night we find a camp site, and exit the sanctuary vessel, have some food and discussion. And set off for day three, about mid-afternoon we can see the tower we explored when we first arrived, at the edge of the magical defences. We see a force of around 20 armed blue-skinned warriors around the base of the tower we explored. Taq looks in windows and sees nothing.

Taq and Barrisso proceed to the keep where we interrogated the book, which is approximately 8km further on. Taq flies in closer to see if the enemy has breached this fortification. Taq sees that there is a group of people moving forward through the spell traps, tightly grouped. It looks like around 21 people, some of whom are the champions, within a 20’ radius – so they may be using the anti-magic properties to protect themselves against the defences. 

We think our best course of action is to land our party, minus Barrisso, in cover to fire missile weapons with poison against the champions. Maybe it is better to do this in the keep, where we have walls and arrow slits, rather than land in the middle of the magic protections, that could also impact us. We decide on the former. As we pass over the group we estimate they’ll reach the edge of the zone of defence in around an hour.

We proceed, maybe twice as fast, with Barrisso going at full speed. He advises the defenders that we need to maintain non-magical defences until their magical defence expires. He sees Real on the battlements, advising him to retreat to avoid being dispelled from this world. We learned in Kardaram that we need to avoid the use of magic, and rely on mundane defenses – fire traps, and poison.

Our plan is to land in the keep. Come out of the ring. We find some protection and prepare missile weapons with poison. There are three good archers, ten fighters. We distribute the poison to the three archers, Barrisso and Gralok. We plan to have Norgreath cast a Limited Wish Call Lightning around the entrance to the keep. It lasts 10 minutes, but has a fixed zone of impact (under a cloud) of 60’ radius, but can be retargeted at a target in that zone each round, impacting those within 5’ of the target. The three specialist archers are [equipped with three poison doses (3 items each).](2026-01-21.md)
